Storytellers

NFRP World adds three storytellers who know about ninja: missions, ranks, rivals, the Bingo Book, the truce and the war between the villages. Pick one on the storyteller page when you start a new game (or change it later in the storyteller settings). Each plays like a vanilla storyteller underneath, so everything vanilla and your other mods add still happens.

| Storyteller | Plays like | In short | |---|---|---| | Hisame the Chronicler | Cassandra Classic | A story with a shape: a calm first year, then rising arcs and, once the truce ends, war. | | Old Sensei Genkō | Phoebe Chillax | Long peaceful stretches with more training, festivals, masters and missions; trouble one thing at a time. | | Kitsunebi the Trickster | Randy Random | Twists at any moment: ambushes, spies, smugglers, sudden festivals. |

What they do differentlyThe three in numbers
What they do differently
  • Your fame sets the pace. A quiet, unknown village is left alone more: some outside threats pass it by. As your village's renown grows, story arcs come faster and bounty hunters take more interest. (How hard raids hit already follows renown and wealth for every storyteller.)
  • Ninja stories instead of generic ones early on. Mech clusters, manhunter packs, infestations and crashed ship parts are rare in the first months. When one would come, a rogue ninja raid or a monster incursion often comes instead.
  • A ninja calendar.

- Festivals (lantern, harvest moon, first snow, kites and more): a mood lift and a lot of recreation for everyone. - Exams: the chūnin exam and the rank exam come round regularly once you have ninja. - Mission offers come in quiet seasons, when nothing big has hit you for a while. - War: after the one-year truce ends, the villages' war reaches you on a steady rhythm.

  • Story arcs that play out over weeks, one at a time, with room to breathe between them:

- A rival returns with a grudge (sometimes alone, for a duel). If they get away again, a scout traces them to their camp, which you can raid. - Bounty hunters come for your most famous wanted ninja. - A deserter from another village asks to join you. A week or two later their village sends hunter-nin after them, unless that village is your ally. Sometimes the deserter was a spy all along. - Rogue ninja with no village pick you as easy prey. - Summon beasts gone wild roam into your land. - Smugglers: a Black Ink courier dumps a stash of silver and goods near your village. Black Ink comes back for it a few days later. - A wandering master visits to teach (the Old Sensei's favourite).

  • No ninja yet? The ninja-only events (exams, rivals) wait, deserters come more often (they bring you a ninja), and everything vanilla carries on as usual.
  • The truce: rivals, bounty hunters and Black Ink don't come during the truce. Hunter-nin chasing a deserter are the exception, as always.
The three in numbers

| | Chronicler | Old Sensei | Trickster | |---|---|---|---| | Festivals | every 30 days | every 15 days | about every 25 days, at random | | Exams | every 30 days | every 20 days | about every 30 days, at random | | War battles after the truce | every 30 days | every 45 days | about every 25 days, at random | | First story arc | day 12 | day 20 | day 8 | | Days between arcs | 12–20 | 20–30 | 6–25 | | Deserter turns out a spy | sometimes | never | often | | Early generic threats | rare for 60 days | rare for 90 days | less common for 30 days |

Difficulty still decides how hard everything hits. On Peaceful, no threat arcs run.
