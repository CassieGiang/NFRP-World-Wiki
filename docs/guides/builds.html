Ninja builds

How the build decides what a ninja can learn, after NFRP's Specialist rules.

In short

A build is 2 Specialists and 2 Non-Specialists, or 1 Master and 1 Non-Specialist. Mastered trees reach S rank, other build trees stop at B. Your own clan trees are free, and the first build is free; changing it later takes a Revamp Scroll.

The build window

The build window is a one-page planner. Each kind (Ninjutsu, Genjutsu, Taijutsu, Clan, Medical) has a row with four buttons: None, Non-Specialist, Specialist and Master. Picking a Master switches the build to the Master layout and clears Specialists; picking a Specialist switches it back. Medical has no Non-Specialist or Master form, so those buttons are greyed out.

Next to each row a rank strip from D to S shows how far that kind can go: green ranks are reachable, gold ranks need a tree you master, grey ranks are out of reach. Under the rows you see the slot counts, the universal ninjutsu and taijutsu caps as strips, and whether the build is complete. On a respec, an If you confirm box lists what would be forgotten and what the respec costs. The full rules are behind Show rules.

Layouts
  • 2 Specialists + 2 Non-Specialists, or 1 Master + 1 Non-Specialist.
  • The categories are Ninjutsu, Genjutsu, Taijutsu, Clan and Medical.
  • A Specialist category can be entered freely and some of its trees can be mastered. A Master category masters more trees. A Non-Specialist category can be entered but its trees stay low.
Ranks
  • Mastered trees go up to S rank. Other trees in a build category stop at B rank.
  • Your own clan trees are always free and don't count against the build.
  • Medical arts stop at D rank unless Medical is part of the build.
  • Jack of Trades raises universal ninjutsu one step. Grand Master lets a pawn master a tree outside its build at the cost of a mastery.
Changing a build
  • The first build is free. Changing it later uses a Revamp Scroll, sold by the Venator or written at a scribe desk.
  • Only the player's pawns are limited by builds. NPCs follow their rank instead.
