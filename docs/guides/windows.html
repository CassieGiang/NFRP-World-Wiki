Windows at a glance

Every NFRP World window has a clickable mockup on the guide that explains it. Click the tabs and buttons in a mockup to see each page, the way the window works in game.

On the bottom barOn a selected ninjaFrom letters and buildingsMockups
On the bottom bar
  • Ninja world: overview, villages and diplomacy, the Bingo Book, missions, news and research, with the windows behind its buttons (send a squad, the live mission, records, teams, diplomacy). See living world.
  • Signature techniques: your techniques, the parts library and known enemy techniques, plus the inspiration and the design editor. See signature techniques.
On a selected ninja
  • Shinobi tab: rank, fame, nickname, stance, kit, team, break meter, rivals, deeds and relics. See fame, Bingo Book and rivals.
  • Ninja build: the build planner, masteries and respec. See builds.
  • Combo and jutsu chain: the break combo order and the jutsu chain. See jutsu chains and solo combos.
  • Kit: pick or edit a tool kit, and the sidearm. See ninja tool kit.
From letters and buildings
  • Send a squad and the live mission: see live squad missions.
  • Chūnin exam: pick the genin to send. See ranks.
  • Leaders' summit: propose alliances and ceasefires. See factions.
  • Inscribe seal at the scribe desk: see seals.
  • The in-game guide: see getting started.

NFRP World is a RimWorld mod by Cassie, an add-on for World of Naruto and Taran Magic Framework. Technique and clan material from the Naruto Forum Role-play (NFRP) community archive. The Kazeraikou clan was created by Marii from NFRP and is used with her permission. This wiki is generated from the mod's files.
