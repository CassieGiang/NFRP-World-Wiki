Summons, contracts and companions

Golems, fabled animals, a bone steed and animals sealed into scrolls.

Element summonsFabled animalsWeapon scrollsCapturing animals
Element summons
  • Golem (Earth C/B/A): one, two or three stone golems. Needs bare ground underfoot, not a built floor. Golem Stone Prayer crushes one enemy; Living Golem Armour puts a stone suit on an ally.
  • Flame Golem (Fire C to S), Water Beast (Water B, needs rain or water within 8 cells), Wood Golem (Wood S, weak to fire), Spontaneous Tree (Wood A, shelters an ally).
  • Bone Golem (Kaguya A) stays until destroyed. Skeletal Steed (Kaguya C) is a fast bone mount that can be ridden through Giddy-Up.
Fabled animals
  • Learn Blood Pact (Summoning arts, D) and pick one contract: Gale Hawks, Iron Boars, Moon Wolves or Stone Tortoises. One contract per ninja.
  • Kuchiyose: Fabled Animal calls animals of that contract: D one young animal, C two, B one great animal, A two, S one giant elder.
Weapon scrolls
  • The Summoning arts tree also has a dozen weapon-scroll attacks, from Hidden Attack and Scroll Whip to Twin Rising Scrolls and Scroll Storm. Most of them hit everyone in the area.
Capturing animals
  • Monster Trainers learn Capture Seal (C/B/A). Weaken a wild animal below half health, or down it, then seal it into a capture scroll. Using the scroll later releases the animal, tamed and bonded to the user.
