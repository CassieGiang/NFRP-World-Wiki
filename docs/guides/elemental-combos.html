Elemental combos

Two ninja hitting the same spot with different elements mix them into a combo.

When two of your ninja hit the same spot with different elements within about two seconds, the elements mix into a combo. It works with area techniques and single-target strikes alike, from any tree. Both casters must be in the same faction. A combo shows its name over the spot and hits everyone in the area (about 3 cells across each way) except the two ninja who made it, so mind your own people. Chain lightning is the exception: it only jumps between enemies. After a combo, the same area can't combo again for a few seconds.

Blade techniques count as wind, and dust as earth.

The combos
  • Fire + wind: Firestorm. 9 flame damage, and a few small fires start.
  • Water + lightning: Electrified water. 6 lightning damage and a stun of about 2.5 seconds.
  • Earth + water: Mudslide. 4 blunt damage, and everyone is stuck in mud (40% slower) for 20 seconds.
  • Wind + water: Squall. 5 blunt damage, and everyone is knocked back 2 cells.
  • Fire + water: Scalding steam. 5 burn damage and a cloud of blinding steam.
  • Lightning + wind: Chain lightning. Jumps through up to 5 nearby enemies, 10 lightning damage falling to 6, with a short stun.
  • Earth + fire: Magma burst. 11 flame damage, and small fires in the middle.
  • Ice + wind: Blizzard. 5 frostbite damage, and everyone is chilled (30% slower, 15% less manipulation) for 25 seconds.
  • Sand + wind: Sandstorm. 4 cut damage and a cloud of blinding sand.
