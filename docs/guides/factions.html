Factions and missions

Who's out there, the war, the truce and ranked missions.

The factions

  • Main villages: Haigakure (fire and earth), Nagisagakure (water and ice), Sajingakure (wind, sand and lightning).
  • Minor villages: Kaedegakure (wood and earth), Otowagakure (sound and genjutsu), Tetsugakure (iron and bone).
  • The Black Ink Syndicate: missing-nin, smugglers and spies.
  • The Order of the Venator: witch-hunters, friendly, and traders of seals, Revamp Scrolls, talismans, potions and scrolls.
  • Feudal houses Akagane, Shiranami and Kurogane: samurai in plate armour, neutral.

War and truce

  • The villages and the Syndicate start hostile to you and to each other. Goodwill can still be raised normally.
  • A one-year truce (60 days) blocks their raids on a new colony. An alert counts down; letters mark its start and end.

Ranked missions

  • The feudal houses, the Venator, and any village or the Syndicate at goodwill 25 or more offer D to S rank missions against a camp of a common enemy.
  • Rewards: the usual payment plus a bonus on success: a technique scroll and seals of the mission's rank, and sometimes a clan weapon at A or S rank.

Enemy ninja

  • NPC ninja learn techniques from their clan and village trees up to their rank: genin C, chunin B, jounin and ANBU A, village heads S.
