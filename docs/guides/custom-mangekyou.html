Custom Mangekyō

A ninja whose Sharingan becomes a Mangekyō can keep the eye World of Naruto gives them, or shape their own: one power in each eye, a power for both eyes, and the look of each eye.

In short

When a colonist's Mangekyō awakens, a letter offers to keep the canon eye or shape your own: two different eye powers (30 types, 90 forms), a joint power (Susanoo with one of four weapons, Izanami, or one of 435 combined arts so far), and for each eye one of 40 patterns and any colour. An Eternal Mangekyō merges in a second pattern. Strain is World of Naruto's own. About 1 enemy Mangekyō user in 100 has a shaped eye.

Awakening
  • The Mangekyō awakens as World of Naruto decides, and World of Naruto picks a canon eye for it (Itachi's, Sasuke's, Shisui's, Obito's and the others).
  • For a colonist, a letter then asks: Shape my own Mangekyō or Keep the eye that awakened. Until you choose, the canon eye stays.
  • Shaping opens the Shape your Mangekyō window. A shaped eye can't be changed afterwards. Any canon Mangekyō technique already learned goes, and its Training Points come back.
  • Eyes that come from a World of Naruto Mangekyō gene (the canon characters) always keep their own eye.
  • Enemies: about 1 Mangekyō user in 100 has a shaped eye, rolled at random.
  • A shaped eye counts as a Mangekyō for the Rinnegan.
The window
  • Left eye and right eye: pick a power type for each (the two must differ), then one of that type's forms. Hover a type or a form to read it.
  • Both eyes: the combined art for your two types, Susanoo with a weapon, or Izanami. Combined arts exist so far for every pair among the first 30 types (Black flames to Mark); pairs with or a later type get theirs in later updates and meanwhile take Susanoo or Izanami.
  • Pattern and colour: choose for both eyes at once or for each eye on its own. 40 patterns; 8 colour presets or any colour with the hue, colour and brightness sliders. The eyes show above the ninja when the Mangekyō opens and on the Mangekyō button, and Susanoo takes the colour.
Eternal Mangekyō

When a shaped eye becomes Eternal (World of Naruto's Eternal Mangekyō), a letter asks for a second pattern. It merges into both eyes for good. Enemies merge one at random.

Using them
  • The techniques work like World of Naruto's own Mangekyō techniques: the Mangekyō must be open, the eyes must still see, and each cast costs eye sight (2% for most, more for the heaviest). An Eternal Mangekyō or the Rinnegan removes the strain, as in World of Naruto.
  • Only the shaping teaches them: the Sharingan can't copy them, and other ninja can't learn them.
  • Susanoo weapons work only while Susanoo stands around the user. Every Mangekyō user still has World of Naruto's Susanoo.
