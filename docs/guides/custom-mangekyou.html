Custom Mangekyō

A ninja whose Sharingan becomes a Mangekyō can keep the eye World of Naruto gives them, or shape their own: one power in each eye, a power for both eyes, a pattern and a colour.

In short

When a colonist's Mangekyō awakens, a letter offers to keep the canon eye or shape your own: two different eye powers (10 types, 26 forms), a joint power (Susanoo with one of four weapons, Izanami, or one of 45 combined arts), 20 patterns and 8 colours. Strain is World of Naruto's own. About 1 enemy Mangekyō user in 100 has an own-shaped eye.

Awakening
  • The Mangekyō awakens as World of Naruto decides, and World of Naruto picks a canon eye for it (Itachi's, Sasuke's, Shisui's, Obito's and the others).
  • For a colonist, a letter then asks: Shape my own Mangekyō or Keep the eye that awakened. Until you choose, the canon eye stays.
  • Shaping opens the Shape your Mangekyō window. A shaped eye can't be changed afterwards. Any canon Mangekyō technique already learned goes, and its Training Points come back.
  • Eyes that come from a World of Naruto Mangekyō gene (the canon characters) always keep their own eye.
  • Enemies: about 1 Mangekyō user in 100 has an own-shaped eye, rolled at random.
  • A shaped eye counts as a Mangekyō for the Rinnegan.
The window
  • Left eye and right eye: pick a power type for each (the two must differ), then one of that type's forms. Hover a type or a form to read it.
  • Both eyes: a combined art (the one for your two types), Susanoo with a weapon, or Izanami.
  • Pattern and colour: 20 patterns and 8 colours (crimson, ember, violet, azure, jade, gold, silver, blood). They show on the eyes, on the Mangekyō button, and Susanoo takes the colour.
Using them
  • The three techniques work like World of Naruto's own Mangekyō techniques: the Mangekyō must be open, the eyes must still see, and each cast costs eye sight (2% for most, more for the heaviest). An Eternal Mangekyō or the Rinnegan removes the strain, as in World of Naruto.
  • Only the shaping teaches them: the Sharingan can't copy them, and other ninja can't learn them.
  • Susanoo weapons work only while Susanoo stands around the user. Every Mangekyō user still has World of Naruto's Susanoo.
  • Strength is pitched at the closest canon power: custom black flames are World of Naruto's own black fire, as hot as Amaterasu.
