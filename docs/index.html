NFRP World

A RimWorld mod that brings the clans, bloodlines and techniques of the Naruto Forum Role-play (NFRP) archive into World of Naruto: more than 150 technique trees, ninja builds, hidden villages at war, ranked missions, summons, alchemy, seals and more.

Requires: Harmony, Taran Magic Framework, World of Naruto, loaded in that order, then NFRP World. Giddy-Up is recommended for riding. Works on RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7. New here? Read Getting started, check the recommended mods, learn about mounts and flying, then browse the technique trees, the creatures and the plants.

NFRP World is a RimWorld mod by Cassie, an add-on for World of Naruto and Taran Magic Framework. Technique and clan material from the Naruto Forum Role-play (NFRP) community archive. The Kazeraikou clan was created by Marii from NFRP and is used with her permission. This wiki is generated from the mod's files.
