Enchantment seals

Seals are inked at a crafting spot, tailoring bench or scribe desk and pressed into gear. Weapon seals act on every hit; armour seals help the wearer.

Gear has 1 seal slot, 2 at excellent or masterwork and 3 at legendary. A new seal goes on at C rank at most and then rises one rank at a time. Inscribing needs a minimum ninjutsu level (D 1, C 4, B 8, A 12, S 16); every level above it makes the seal stronger. Seals marked with a technique can only be made by an artisan who knows it (Equipment enhancing tree).
