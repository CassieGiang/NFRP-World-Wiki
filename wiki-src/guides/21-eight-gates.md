# The Eight Celestial Gates
A taijutsu style from the NFRP archive's "Eight Celestial Gates" thread: the user forces open the gates of their own chakra network for a burst of impossible strength, and pays for it afterwards. It sits in the **Taijutsu** tab. The tree starts locked and is learned like the other archive trees: technique scrolls, mission rewards, written scrolls and scroll merchants. It counts toward the ninja build like any Taijutsu tree.

Each gate is named after its chakra wheel. Every gate needs the gate before it, and a higher Taijutsu level. A gate is a short self-buff (about 25 seconds; the Eighth about 20). When it **wears off**, the strain hits: the pawn is slowed and clumsy for hours, and from the Fourth Gate on they lose blood and take damage. Opening the Eighth Gate can kill a pawn who is already hurt.

## The gates
- **Muladhara (First Gate: the Root)**, C rank, level 4: faster, harder melee blows (×1.1), small hit and dodge bonus, a little pain resistance. Afterwards: 1 hour of strain.
- **Swadhisthana (Second Gate: the Sacral)**, B rank, level 6: faster still (×1.15 melee damage), wounds heal four times as fast and stop bleeding. Afterwards: 2 hours of strain.
- **Manipura (Third Gate: the Solar Plexus)**, B rank, level 8: ×1.25 melee damage, better hit and dodge. Afterwards: 5 hours of strain.
- **Anahata (Fourth Gate: the Heart)**, A rank, level 10: ×1.35 melee damage. Afterwards: a day of heavy strain (moving and manipulation down), some blood loss and damage.
- **Vishuddha (Fifth Gate: the Throat)**, A rank, level 12: ×1.5 melee damage. Afterwards: a day of stronger strain, more blood loss and damage.
- **Ajna (Sixth Gate: the Third Eye)**, S rank, level 14: ×1.7 melee damage. Afterwards: a day of strain in which the pawn takes extra damage, heavy blood loss.
- **Sahasrara (Seventh Gate: the Crown)**, S rank, level 16: ×1.9 melee damage. Afterwards: a day and a half of strain, very heavy blood loss.
- **Patala (Eighth Gate: the Underworld)**, S rank, level 18: ×2.4 melee damage, huge speed, hit and dodge, ignores most pain. Afterwards: two days barely able to move, about 60% blood loss and heavy damage. Nearly fatal.

## Gate strikes
All of these need the First Gate learned.

- **Gentle Touch** (C): a stunning palm strike that rarely kills.
- **Stunning Fist** (C): a long stun.
- **Chakra Disruption** (C): the target turns clumsy and inaccurate for a while.
- **Luminosity** (C): a burning, dazzling ray of light from the palm.
- **Aether Ties** (C): the target is slowed.
- **Veil of Sins** (C): a protective veil that also speeds healing.
- **Crack the Shell** (B): a blow that ignores most armour.
- **Seal Wounds** (B): heals the user.
- **Revitalizing Breath** (B): heals a friend.
- **Uncanny Dodge** (B, passive) and **Improved Uncanny Dodge** (A, passive): better dodging.
- **Sever the Flow** (A): cuts the target off from its chakra, so no techniques for a while.
- **Incandescent Strike** (S): a blow of white-hot radiant energy.
- **Wages of Sin** (S): a crushing blow that leaves the target helpless.

Damaging strikes hit anyone in the way, friends included. Technique and style material from the Naruto Forum Role-play (NFRP) community archive.
