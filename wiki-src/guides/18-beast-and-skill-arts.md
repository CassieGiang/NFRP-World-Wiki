# Beast arts and skill techniques
## Beast arts
- **Hive mind**: for the insect-control clan. Linked minds, shared senses, remote control and a chakra conduit through the swarm.
- **Scent**: for the hound clan. Tracking, picking out fear, disease and poison by smell.
- **Web**: for the spider clan. Webs that trap, poison or stun, web lines, protective webs and sticky shelters.
- **Troll** (Taijutsu tab, open to anyone who unlocks it): a fearsome presence, tusks, bursts of vigour, curses and a rain of rocks.

The hive mind, scent and web trees only show for pawns with that clan's gene. They need Ninjutsu levels to learn and train Ninjutsu.

## Skill techniques
Five trees in the Ninjutsu tab that sharpen everyday skills and toughness, most of them passives. **Basic skill techniques** are open to every ninja.

## Black Sun, Veritas and the artisan paths
- **Onimusha no Taiyoushin (Warriors of the Black Sun)**: imprint an enemy with the black sun's mark, then make it flare and burst.
- **Veritas (Truth)**: a seal that makes its target unable to lie. The seal watches them, and the truth flood overwhelms them.
- **Artisan paths**: for crafters who fight. Better work, a jeweller's eye, armour training, a defensive aura, stone-like skin, trap spotting and splintering weapons.
