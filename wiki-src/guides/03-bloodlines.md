# Clans and bloodlines
Clans come from genes, and the only way to take another clan's power is surgery.

@@summary Each clan is a gene with its own tree. Another clan's power comes only from surgery: a Medical Specialist (medical arts B or higher, Medicine 8) extracts tissue and grafts it, capped at B rank. A graft can be rejected; the graft is lost, never the patient.

## Clans
- Each clan is a gene (and a xenotype) with its own tree in the Clan tab: Kazeraikou, Inuzuka, Nara, Kaguya, Masamune (Iron Sand), Hozuki, Akimichi, Yamanaka, Hoshigaki, Ōgumo, Kamizuru, Protean, Toujitakumi, Airontoku and eighteen more from the archive.
- World of Naruto's own Sharingan, Byakugan and Bug Control genes get companion trees: Uchiha arts, Hyuuga arts and Aburame arts. The eye techniques need the eye switched on.
- Crystal Release and Paper Release are genes with ninjutsu trees.

## Kedōin in RimWorld

See the [Kedōin technique tree](../trees/nf-clankedoin.html) for ranks, icons and base costs.

### Body shaping and movement
- **Warped Visage** changes hair and skin, restores the saved appearance and selects the form used by **Flesh Architect**. Compact moves and dodges faster with lighter strikes. Normal keeps the pawn's build and improves melee damage. Hulking gives heavier strikes and blunt resistance at the cost of speed.
- **Shift of Organs** redirects the next hit aimed at an internal part to an outer part. The protection expires after one redirect or half an in-game hour.
- **External Shift** moves one or two clear tiles in a straight line. **Formless Flexion** gives +0.9 move speed and improves melee dodge while active. **Gecko Grip** crosses one constructed wall to a clear tile directly beyond it.
- **Flesh Fabric** changes the displayed colors of worn clothing. **Cowl of Kedōin** gives worn headgear a hood appearance. **Autolytic Control** restores the default look and ends active forms. The equipped items and their stats stay in place.

### Maw techniques and homunculi
- **Legion of Maw** bites one hostile pawn in reach and line of sight. Its C, B and A ranks reach 1.9, 2.9 and 3.9 tiles. Higher ranks can stagger, and Flesh Architect adds 4 bite damage.
- **Maw Savagery** adds 1.5 tiles of reach and raises the chakra cost by 25%. **Enduring Bites** keeps your windup through a hit that deals up to 8 damage. **Mastery of Maws** halves the windup.
- **Craft Lesser Homunculi** summons up to two draftable scouts and haulers per creator for six in-game hours each. **Craft Greater Homunculi** summons one draftable combat guardian for four hours. Dismissing a homunculus drops what it carries.
- **Mirror Function** lets drafted homunculi echo known Kedōin arts. C adds Legion of Maw at reduced damage, B adds Shift of Organs, A adds Formless Flexion and a stronger Maw, and S raises the Maw echo to A rank if the creator knows it. The echoes share a cooldown on each homunculus.
- **Sentient Limbs** gives a 25% chance for an arm, leg, hand or foot severed by a hostile pawn to become a Lesser Homunculus for one in-game hour. This uses one of the two Lesser slots and can happen even if the hit downs the creator.

### Recovery and storage
- **Organ Reattachment** can restore a limb lost within the last in-game hour. If it became a homunculus, bring that summon within three tiles and into sight. Reattachment consumes the summon, and the limb takes another hour to regain full function.
- **Re-mortality Protocol** regrows the largest eligible missing body part in two in-game hours, including older losses. It excludes the head and heart and adds one year of biological age on success.
- **Hyperactive Immunity** purges poison and toxins at C. B adds one hour of toxic resistance, A also reduces immunizable sickness, and S strengthens both the protection and the purge. Existing A-rank mastery carries forward.
- **Throat Pocket** and **Stomach Pocket** each hold one nearby stack, with limits of 0.5 kg and 5 kg respectively. **Corpse Conceal** holds one fresh corpse for an hour. **Organ Host** holds one harvested natural organ for six hours. Target the object to store it, then target yourself to release it. The techniques reject quest-tagged items and bodies.

## Taking a bloodline
- Only a **Medical Specialist** with medical arts at **B rank or higher** and Medicine 8 can do it.
- **Extract bloodline tissue** from a living clan prisoner (painful, a 15% chance of death, and the colony won't like it), or from a fresh clan corpse at the butcher table.
- **Take bloodline eye**: for eye bloodlines (Sharingan, Byakugan, Ryuugan and others), remove the eye itself. The eye is lost for good, but the sample is rejected much less.
- Success: B 40%, A 60%, S 80%, adjusted by Medicine skill. Samples rot after 4 days unless kept cold.
- **Graft bloodline**: gives the clan gene and its tree, capped at **B rank** (A rank when an S-rank Medical Specialist grafts living tissue).

## Rejection
- A graft brings **graft rejection**, an illness that can be tended. If it runs its full course the graft is lost, never the patient.
- One graft per person. A second graft risks losing both.
- Some members of the Black Ink Syndicate carry stolen, grafted bloodlines of their own.
