# Live squad missions

Squad missions (see *The living world*) can now be watched as they happen, and what your ninja can actually do decides how the trip goes.

## Following a trip
When a squad leaves, a letter offers **Watch the mission**. You can also open it from the Ninja world panel (**Watch: …** buttons) at any time while the squad is away.

The window doesn't pause the game. It shows:
- a small map of the road in your home's landscape (forest, snow, desert, jungle or swamp colours),
- the squad moving out to the target and back,
- markers for everything that happened so far, and a log with the day and hour,
- who in the squad is fine or hurt.

Buttons: **Live** follows the trip in game time, **Replay so far** plays what happened again, **Skip to the end** brings the squad home at once (a setting can turn this off), **Mission records** and **Next squad**.

## What decides the trip
Every squad member counts:
- **Rank and level** against the mission's rank decide most fights and the objective.
- **Taijutsu**, **summons** and **genjutsu** help in fights; **fire** users do better against beasts.
- **Sensors** slip around ambushes, find more on the road and can track a hidden enemy camp.
- **Fast**, **sensor** or **sealing** ninja disarm traps.
- **Medics** patch up the wounded on the road (their wounds are halved).

On the road the squad may fight rogue ninja, bandits, beasts, Black Ink crews or someone from the Bingo Book (harder, but beating them pays a share of the bounty), find herbs, silver, gold, jade or steel, meet merchants, pilgrims and refugees, spot a legendary beast or a war, or find a hidden camp you can raid afterwards.

## Coming home
What happened on the road is what they bring back: the wounds, the loot and bounty (by drop pod), a relic if they found one, the hidden camp on the world map, fame for the fights they won, hunger and tiredness, and practice in a couple of skills, on top of the usual mission pay, XP and goodwill.

## Village mission rank
Every mission earns your village points (more for higher ranks and successes). The rank opens harder missions: **B at level 2, A at level 3, S at level 4**, and makes great successes (extra pay) more likely.

## Relics
Rarely, a squad finds one of ten old relics. Each gives everyone in the village a small permanent bonus, shown in the health tab as *village relics*:

| Relic | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Weathered founding scroll | +3% learning |
| Sandals of the mountain pass | +0.05 move speed |
| Crest of the old hearth | slightly harder to break mentally |
| Healer's lotus jar | +3% immunity gain |
| Tempered anvil stone | +2% work speed |
| Moon bell of quiet sleep | +3% rest rate |
| Iron prayer beads | +2% pain shock threshold |
| Third-eye ink stone | +1% consciousness |
| Sealed riverstone blade | melee strikes a little faster |
| Flask of the still spring | +3% chakra regeneration |

## Mission records
The **Mission records** window lists every mission with its full log, your mission rank, totals and relics.

## Settings
Settings → World and events → Living world: **Live squad missions**, **Allow "skip to the end"**, and sliders for rewards, injuries, relic chance and replay speed.
