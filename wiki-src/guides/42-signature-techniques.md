# Signature techniques
Your ninja can create techniques of their own: pick an element, a shape, a few modifiers and a look, give it a name, and develop it at a training spot. Enemy leaders, jōnin and rivals carry signatures of their own too.

## How a ninja gets one
- **On purpose:** select a ninja and press **Design a signature technique**. The design editor opens with every part your village has earned.
- **Inspired:** missions, beating a rival, a new fame tier, opening an old sealed cache, every 20 training sessions, and (with an NFRP storyteller) festivals, wandering masters and quiet moments of insight can inspire a ninja. They get three ideas within their rank. Press **Inspired** to pick one, keep them for later, or let them go.
- **Refining an idea** keeps its shape. You may add one modifier and drop one before you start.
- **Limits:** genin and chūnin may own one signature, jōnin two, village leaders three.

## Developing
- Developing is research-like work at a training spot, one hour at a time. The ninja's **Developing** button shows the progress.
- Time for an inspired idea: D 4 hours, C 6, B 10, A 16, S 24. A design made from scratch takes twice as long.
- A-rank work has an 8% chance per hour to backfire, S-rank 15%. A backfire burns or shocks a hand or arm a little; the progress is kept.
- The rank needs ninjutsu: D 2, C 6, B 10, A 14, S 18. Modifiers: two, plus one for jōnin and one more for leaders.

## Parts
Every village starts with a set of shapes, modifiers and motifs. The rest are **earned**, and the parts window says how.

| Earned by | Some parts it opens |
|---|---|
| C-rank missions | wave, lance, barrier; quick, piercing, pinning, rooting; spiral, turtle shell |
| B-rank missions | pillars, prison, rain; stunning, numbing, paralysing; sharks, hounds, serpent, giant hands |
| A-rank missions | storm, flash step; freezing, lulling, of stealth; dragon, tiger, comet |
| S-rank missions | great beast; sun flare |
| Beating a rival | drill; wolf |
| Becoming Feared | rallying aura, terror genjutsu; terrifying, dreadful; feathers, crane |
| Becoming Legendary | of heroism; crescent moon |
| Opening old seals | confusion genjutsu, vortex; searing, shocking, venomous; petals, bones, spider web, mirror shards |
| Training | dash strike; lingering; lotus |

- **Shapes** (24) decide what the technique does: a bullet, a breath, a field, a bind, a barrier, a mending, a curse and more.
- **Modifiers** (34) tune it: stronger, farther, wider, cheaper, and effects such as stunning, freezing, chakra-sealing or of swiftness.
- **Motifs** (22 besides plain) decide its look: dragons, birds, hounds, sharks, a serpent, a crane, a wolf, a comet, a lotus, a crescent moon and more. Each is painted art that takes the element's colour.

## Teaching and scrolls
- The creator can write the technique on a scroll at the scribe desk. Another of your ninja who reads it starts **Learning** it at a training spot.
- The creator casts it a little stronger (+15%); someone who learned it from a scroll a little weaker (−15%).
- **No copying.** The Sharingan and any other copy effect can't take a signature technique. Only scrolls teach it.

## Enemy signatures
- Village leaders always carry one, jōnin often, and a rival may come back from training with one.
- You only learn an enemy's technique once your colony sees it used. It then shows in the **Known enemy techniques** tab and in the Bingo Book.
- When the creator dies and nobody else knows it, the technique is gone.

## Where to find them
- **Signature techniques** on the bottom bar: your techniques, the parts library and known enemy techniques.
- The technique tree shows only the selected ninja's signatures.
- **Write art requests** saves a list of signatures that would look better with their own painted art.
