# Ninja builds
How the build decides what a ninja can learn, after NFRP's Specialist rules.

@@summary A build is 2 Specialists and 2 Non-Specialists, or 1 Master and 1 Non-Specialist. It decides which trees a ninja may enter and how many Training Points each may hold; mastered trees have no cap. Medical arts need a Medical Specialist. Masteries can be traded for Grand Master, Jack of Trades or a profession (Alchemist, Artisan, Ritualist).

## The build window
The build window is a one-page planner. Each kind (Ninjutsu, Genjutsu, Taijutsu, Clan, Medical) has a row with four buttons: **None**, **Non-Specialist**, **Specialist** and **Master**. Picking a Master switches the build to the Master layout and clears Specialists; picking a Specialist switches it back. Medical has no Non-Specialist or Master form, so those buttons are greyed out.

Next to each row a **rank strip** from D to S shows how far that kind can go: green ranks are reachable, gold ranks need a tree you master, grey ranks are out of reach. Under the rows you see the slot counts, the universal ninjutsu and taijutsu caps as strips, and whether the build is complete. On a respec, an **If you confirm** box lists what would be forgotten and what the respec costs. The full rules are behind **Show rules**.

## Layouts
- **2 Specialists + 2 Non-Specialists**, or **1 Master + 1 Non-Specialist**.
- The categories are Ninjutsu, Genjutsu, Taijutsu, Clan and Medical.
- A Specialist category can be entered freely and some of its trees can be **mastered**. A Master category masters more trees. A Non-Specialist category can be entered but its trees stay low.

## What the build allows
- Mastered trees go up to **S rank** and hold any number of [Training Points](training-points.html). An unmastered tree holds **450 TP** (Master), **400** (Specialist) or **350** (Non-Specialist); a Master's extra slots 350. A tree past its cap uses a free mastery if there is one.
- The universal ninjutsu, taijutsu and genjutsu pools hold 800 (Master), 600 (Specialist), 400 (Non-Specialist) or 200 TP with no pick.
- Your own clan or bloodline trees are always free, count as mastered and don't count against the build.
- **Academy techniques** never count toward the build.
- **Medical arts** (Medical Ninjutsu, Sensor, Soulcaster) need a **Medical Specialist**: without one, not even D rank.
- Some trees also need a [tree scroll](tree-scrolls.html), a bloodline or a title before the build counts them.

## Miscellaneous: trading a mastery
Under **Miscellaneous (trade a mastery)** a free mastery can become:
- **Grand Master** (up to 3): master a tree you normally couldn't.
- **Jack of Trades** (once): universal ninjutsu one rank higher.
- **Alchemist**, **Artisan** or **Ritualist** (one per ritual school): the professions NFRP puts on Miscellaneous slots. See [Professions](professions.html#alchemist-artisan-and-ritualist).

## Changing a build
- The first build is free. Changing it later uses a **Revamp Scroll**, sold by the Venator or written at a scribe desk.
- A respec gives back, in full, the TP spent on techniques the new build drops.
- Only the player's pawns are limited by builds. NPCs buy techniques from a TP budget by rank.
