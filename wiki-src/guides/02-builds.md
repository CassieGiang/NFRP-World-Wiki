# Ninja builds
How the build decides what a ninja can learn, after NFRP's Specialist rules.

## Layouts
- **2 Specialists + 2 Non-Specialists**, or **1 Master + 1 Non-Specialist**.
- The categories are Ninjutsu, Genjutsu, Taijutsu, Clan and Medical.
- A Specialist category can be entered freely and some of its trees can be **mastered**. A Master category masters more trees. A Non-Specialist category can be entered but its trees stay low.

## Ranks
- Mastered trees go up to **S rank**. Other trees in a build category stop at **B rank**.
- Your own clan trees are always free and don't count against the build.
- Medical arts stop at D rank unless Medical is part of the build.
- **Jack of Trades** raises universal ninjutsu one step. **Grand Master** lets a pawn master a tree outside its build at the cost of a mastery.

## Changing a build
- The first build is free. Changing it later uses a **Revamp Scroll**, sold by the Venator or written at a scribe desk.
- Only the player's pawns are limited by builds. NPCs follow their rank instead.
