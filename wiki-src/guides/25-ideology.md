# Ideology
With the Ideology DLC, NFRP World adds five ninja memes, eight ninja issues with their precepts, three roles, and a creed for every NFRP faction. Without the DLC none of this loads.

@@summary With Ideology: five ninja memes, ninja issues with their precepts, three roles, a creed for every NFRP faction and four ninja rituals. Without the DLC none of it loads.

## Memes
- **Shinobi Code**: a ninja lives by rank, orders and the mission. Duty to the village comes before the self.
- **Clan Honour**: blood and name are sacred. A clan's techniques belong to the clan, and leaving your people is unforgivable.
- **Chakra Reverence**: chakra is the breath of the world. Training it is a sacred act, and the body is a vessel to be honed.
- **The Forbidden Path**: power is power. Grafted bloodlines, opened gates and broken oaths are just tools.
- **Sage of the Wilds**: true strength flows from the land, the beasts and the old places.

## Issues and precepts
- **Ninja rank**: revered, earned or meaningless. Decides how proud ranked ninja feel, how small the unranked feel and how much a promotion is celebrated.
- **Masked duty**: required (every ranked ninja must wear a masked-guard mask), honoured or distrusted.
- **Inner gates**: revered, accepted or forbidden. Opening one of the [Eight Gates](eight-gates.html) is a moment of pride, nothing special, or a terrible act.
- **Leaving the village**: betrayal, understood or freedom. Covers ninja who become missing-nin after you leave a sworn village.
- **Sparring**: sacred duty (more than ten days without a session feels like neglect), valued or indifferent.
- **Bloodline grafting**: abhorrent, disapproved, acceptable or revered.
- **Ninja missions**: sacred oath, pragmatic or mercenary. Decides how good a completed mission feels and how much a failed one stings.

## Roles
- **Sensei**: a student training near the sensei earns 19 TP a session instead of 15 (25% more experience without Training Points), and the sensei learns a little faster too.
- **Village guardian**: takes less damage and moves a little faster.
- **Shrine keeper**: speaks with gentle authority and is hard to rattle.

## Faction creeds
- **Haigakure**: Shinobi Code and Clan Honour.
- **Nagisagakure**: Shinobi Code and Sage of the Wilds.
- **Sajingakure**: Shinobi Code and Chakra Reverence.
- **Kaedegakure, Otowagakure and Tetsugakure**: Shinobi Code.
- **The Black Ink Syndicate**: The Forbidden Path.
- **The Cult of Jashin**: Jashin.
- **The Order of the Venator**: Sage of the Wilds.
- **The feudal houses** Akagane, Shiranami and Kurogane: Clan Honour.

## Rituals
Four ninja rituals, each brought by one of the memes and open to ideoligions with a related meme:
- **Rank ceremony** (Shinobi Code): the leader honours a ranked ninja. Good mood and 10 TP for the honoree (5 for a decent ceremony; class experience without Training Points).
- **Memorial for the fallen** (Clan Honour): held at a grave. If the dead was a ninja, the ninja present gain 10 TP (5 for a decent memorial; experience without Training Points).
- **Festival rite** (Sage of the Wilds): during a village festival it raises goodwill with the host village.
- **Blood pact rite** (The Forbidden Path): the pact-bearer regains chakra faster for one to three days, longer and stronger with a summoning contract.

An ideoligion made before the rituals were added gets them only after it is reformed.

## Jashinism
**Jashin** is a meme of its own: Jashin demands slaughter, and pain is a prayer. An ideoligion with it always holds:
- **Pain: idealized** (pain lifts the mood).
- **Jashin's offering: required.** Every person a believer kills in melee is an offering (a good mood for 4 days); more than ten days without one is a bad mood.
- **Jashin's rite**, a ritual led by a Jashinist at a ritual spot or altar, with a person killed within the last day lying within 6 cells and a colonist of another ideoligion listening. A fervent or ecstatic rite converts them; a cold one shakes their certainty.

Jashinism is rare when ideoligions are made at random, and a small hostile **Cult of Jashin** lives in every world: a world made before the cult existed gets it, with one shrine, the next time the save is loaded. Followers are not immortal. Only a follower can use the three-bladed scythe's Jashin Ritual: cut a foe, stand in the circle of their blood, and for 30 seconds every wound you take there is dealt to them as well.
