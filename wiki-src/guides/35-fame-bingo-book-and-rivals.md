# Fame, the Bingo Book and rivals
Ninja build a reputation from what they do. Famous ninja lift their allies and shake their enemies, earn a nickname from what they are known for, end up in the Bingo Book with a price on their head, and make enemies who come back for them.

## Fame
Every ninja has fame, yours and the enemy's. It comes from deeds:
- **kills**, worth far more for high ranks (a village leader is worth eight times a genin), plus a share of the victim's own fame
- **breaks** and **combos**, more if the finisher kills
- **winning a fight**: everyone who fought gets fame when the map is clear of enemies again, more for big fights
- **missions** (D to S rank), **promotions**, and **beating a rival**
- **S and A-rank techniques used in a fight** (only a little a day, so repeating one doesn't buy fame)

Fame slowly fades when nothing happens, but never below half of the best tier the ninja ever reached: a legend stays at least feared. Being downed, captured or fleeing a fight in a panic costs some fame.

| Tier | Fame |
|---|---|
| Unknown | below 60 |
| Known | 60 |
| Feared | 250 |
| Legendary | 800 |

Enemy ninja come with the fame of their rank: a genin is rarely known, many jōnin are, village leaders are famous everywhere.

## Inspire and intimidate
During a fight, a Known, Feared or Legendary ninja affects everyone within 9 cells:
- **allies are inspired**: a little better aim, hit chance and dodge, a steadier mind, and a mood boost ("fighting beside a legend")
- **enemies are intimidated**: a little worse aim, hit chance and dodge, a shakier mind and a mood penalty, unless they are at least as famous themselves

The effect grows with the tier (2%, 4% or 6%). It also changes **break meters**: famous attackers fill them faster, intimidated fighters break faster and inspired ones slower. Weak, unranked enemy fighters near a Feared or Legendary ninja may panic and run.

## Nicknames
When a ninja becomes Known, people start calling them by a nickname taken from what they are known for, and it gets grander at Feared and Legendary. A lightning ninja might be "Thunderclap", then "Stormcaller", then "the Living Storm"; a ninja who knows the arts of many schools "Mirror", then "the Borrower"; a ninja forever dashing and vanishing "Blink", then "the Vanishing Light". Healers, illusionists, sealers, summoners, close fighters, swordsmen, hunters of high-ranked ninja, survivors, the never-wounded, guardians, mission-runners and rival-beaters all have their own names.

A letter tells you when one of your ninja earns a nickname. **A nickname you set yourself is never replaced**: you get a letter offering the new one instead. Enemy ninja have nicknames too.

## The Bingo Book
Open it with the **Bingo Book** button at the top of the Ninja world panel. It lists the most famous ninja your colony knows of: your own, the enemies you have fought, village leaders and your rivals, sorted by fame. Each page shows their rank and village, their fame, what they are known for, their **bounty** (3 silver per point of fame, dead or alive), who wants them, where they were last seen and their deeds.

**Your famous ninja are hunted.** Once one of your ninja is Known and some faction is hostile to you, bounty hunters from a hostile village or the Black Ink Syndicate may come for them, more often and stronger the more famous they are (never during the truce, at least 20 days apart). The hunters go for their target first.

**You can hunt.** On an enemy's page, **Hunt** posts a mission: a camp of their faction a few days away with the target in it. Kill or capture the target and the bounty arrives by drop pod. If they get away, the bounty stays open. Up to three hunts can be open at once.

## Rivals
An enemy who gets away after a fight with you can become a **rival**:
- always if they killed or downed one of yours, often if you broke them and they still escaped, sometimes after a hard fight; ninja much more than ordinary raiders
- they hate one of your ninja: the one who broke them or hurt them most
- they learn from what beat them: they resist the kind of damage that hurt them most, get harder to break, or get used to being outnumbered
- they carry **battle scars** naming who gave them
- while away they grow: more skill, promotions, more fame

Rivals come back leading a raid, or send a **duel challenge** to the ninja they hate (see [Reputation, duels and intel](reputation-duels-and-intel.html)), and go for that ninja first. A feared rival "killed" far from your home may turn out to have survived and return in a mask. A captured rival is harder to recruit; recruiting one ends the rivalry. A famous colonist taken by an enemy faction can come back as a rival too.

Rivals have a gold "rival" tag in the Bingo Book, with their story on their page.

## Settings
Under **Village life → Fame, Bingo Book and Rivals**:
- **Fame** on or off
- **Inspire and intimidate strength** (0 to 200%)
- **Weak enemies may flee from feared ninja**
- **Earned nicknames**: Apply, Suggest or Off
- **Bingo Book hunters** on or off, and how often
- **Rivals** on or off, the most rivals at once (default 6), and how often they return

More parts (witnesses, masked names, infamy, renown, duels, legacy, bounties on bodies, intel and interrogation, beheading a village) are in [Reputation, duels and intel](reputation-duels-and-intel.html).
