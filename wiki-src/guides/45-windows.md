# Windows at a glance
Every NFRP World window has a clickable mockup on the guide that explains it. Click the tabs and buttons in a mockup to see each page, the way the window works in game.

## On the bottom bar
- **Ninja world:** overview, villages and diplomacy, the Bingo Book, missions, news and research, with the windows behind its buttons (send a squad, the live mission, records, teams, diplomacy). See [living world](living-world.html).
- **Signature techniques:** your techniques, the parts library and known enemy techniques, plus the inspiration and the design editor. See [signature techniques](signature-techniques.html).

## On a selected ninja
- **Shinobi tab:** rank, fame, nickname, stance, kit, team, break meter, rivals, deeds and relics. See [fame, Bingo Book and rivals](fame-bingo-book-and-rivals.html).
- **Ninja build:** the build planner, masteries and respec. See [builds](builds.html).
- **Combo and jutsu chain:** the break combo order and the jutsu chain. See [jutsu chains and solo combos](jutsu-chains-and-solo-combos.html).
- **Kit:** pick or edit a tool kit, and the sidearm. See [ninja tool kit](ninja-tool-kit.html).

## From letters and buildings
- **Send a squad and the live mission:** see [live squad missions](live-squad-missions.html).
- **Chūnin exam:** pick the genin to send. See [ranks](ranks.html).
- **Leaders' summit:** propose alliances and ceasefires. See [factions](factions.html).
- **Inscribe seal** at the scribe desk: see [seals](seals.html).
- **The in-game guide:** see [getting started](getting-started.html).

## Mockups
Example names and numbers in the mockups are made up; labels, buttons and rules come from the mod.
