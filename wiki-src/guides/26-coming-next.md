# Coming in the next update
What the next update adds. It is still being built, so details may change during testing.

## Riding and flying
- NFRP World's big tame beasts and the new creatures become mounts through Giddy-Up, like other ridable animals: workers ride them to far jobs, and fighters can ride them into battle.
- A pawn riding one of the flying creatures gets a **Fly** order: the pair takes off, flies over walls and water, and lands still mounted.
- Caravans in which everyone rides a flyer may travel faster on the world map.

## New creatures
- **Ziz**, a huge storm bird of wind and lightning, rare and hard to tame.
- More **couatls** beside the jet couatl: the rainbow-feathered common couatl (a healer), the primal couatl (its gaze stuns) and the radiant couatl (light, and a cursing bite), plus the smaller **pied viper**.
- **Bulettes**, burrowing land sharks that vanish underground and burst up under their prey: common, hammerhead and the acid-slimed xenarth.

## Beast arts
- Creatures get **signature attacks** of their own, so a legendary beast fights like one.
