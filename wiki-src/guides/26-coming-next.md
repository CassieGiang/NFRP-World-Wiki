# Coming in the next update
What the next update adds. It is built but not released yet, so details may still change during testing.

## Required mod
- **[Giddy-Up](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3674332861)** becomes a required mod. Load order: Harmony, Taran Magic Framework, World of Naruto, Giddy-Up, NFRP World.

## Riding and flying
- NFRP World's big tame beasts and new creatures can be ridden through Giddy-Up, like other mounts: workers ride them to far jobs, and fighters can ride them into battle.
- A pawn riding one of the flying creatures gets **Fly to**: the pair takes off, flies over walls and water, and lands still mounted.

## New creatures
- **Ziz**, a huge storm bird of wind and lightning, rare and hard to tame.
- More **couatls** beside the jet couatl: the rainbow-feathered common couatl (a healer), the primal couatl (its gaze stuns) and the radiant couatl (light, a cursing bite), plus the smaller **pied viper**.
- **Bulettes**, burrowing land sharks that vanish underground and burst up under their prey: common, hammerhead and the acid-slimed xenarth.

## Leader endgame and the war
- **Found your own hidden village** from the ninja world panel once a checklist is met: a village leader, three more chūnin or higher, six colonists, friendly villages, a shrine, a training building and a comms console. Other villages send you mission requests afterwards.
- **Leaders' summits** every 60 days: propose alliances and ceasefires, and call a united front against the Black Ink Syndicate. Four partners and a united front, or a Syndicate with no settlements left, ends the war.
- **A visible war**: villages take each other's settlements, with news letters. Now and then two squads fight near your home and you can take a side or stay out.

## Growing up a ninja
- **Academy** (Biotech): children gain class experience from their lessons and sit an easier graduation exam to become genin at 13.
- **Japanese names**: tree and technique names read Japanese first, English in brackets. Kawarimi no Jutsu becomes Irekawari (body switch).
- **A-rank scrolls**: a village you're sworn to sells scrolls up to A rank when a jōnin or the village leader does the talking.
- **Onboarding**: five short tip letters over the first days and a "getting started" list on the ninja world panel. Both can be switched off.

## Ninja around you
- **Squad roles** for NPC ninja: frontline, medic (heals allies), sensor (marks targets) and support.
- **Rituals** for Ideology: rank ceremony, memorial, festival rite and blood pact. See [Ideology](ideology.html).

## Looks and sound
- **Smoother effects**: technique animations blend from frame to frame, the most used ones are repainted with eight frames, every effect gets a soft glow in its element's colour, lightning leaves an afterglow and projectiles leave fading trails. Each can be switched off.
- **Creatures come alive**: flyers hover and bob (the main flyers flap their wings), serpents sway, big beasts breathe, spirits drift and flicker; magical creatures get element auras and attack flashes. Each can be switched off.
- **Sound cues**: an element sound when a technique starts, and sounds for gates, summoning, promotions, festivals and combos.

- A startup log line and a dev action that lists every Harmony patch NFRP World applies. See [For modders](for-modders.html).
