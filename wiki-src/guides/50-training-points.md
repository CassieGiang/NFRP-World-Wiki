# Training Points
Every ninja of yours has a pool of **Training Points (TP)**, as in NFRP. TP set the ninja's class (D to S) and pay for techniques, which you choose yourself in the **Learn techniques** window.

@@summary One pool of Training Points per ninja sets the class (D, C at 500, B at 1,500, A at 3,000, S at 5,500) and pays for techniques at NFRP's prices (E 5, D and C 10, B 40, A 80, S 100). TP come from training, classes, missions, exams, fights and a little casting. Techniques are bought in the Learn techniques window and never learn themselves.

## Class
The total TP a ninja has ever earned sets the class. Spending never lowers it.

| Class | Total TP | Learns up to |
|---|---|---|
| D | 0 (start: 150, or 100 with a bloodline; a backstory rank brings more, see [Names and backstories](names-and-backstories.html#rank-from-the-backstory)) | B rank (E, D, C, B) |
| C | 500 | A rank |
| B | 1,500 | S rank |
| A | 3,000 | nothing new on the technique side |
| S | 5,500 | nothing new on the technique side |

A new ninja gets the starting TP when their ninja build is chosen. World of Naruto's class levels now follow the class (weighted by the build: full for its Specialist and Master kinds, about two thirds for Non-Specialist kinds, a third for the rest), so its chakra, casting and melee bonuses keep working. Casting no longer raises those levels.

## Prices
E 5, D 10, C 10, B 40, A 80, S 100 TP, the same at every class. A technique with several ranks is bought one rank at a time from its lowest.
- **Free:** Academy techniques for genin and up (see [Ninja ranks](ranks.html)); the eye itself (Sharingan, Byakugan) and other techniques that come with a gene, trait or item; your signature techniques; a weapon's own technique.
- **Technique scrolls**, **written scrolls** and grimoires teach at half price (the scroll or grimoire says up front if the pool is short); teachers and wandering masters charge the full price. Under Training Points a written scroll has no class level gate: the class, the TP and any prerequisite decide.
- **Prerequisites:** a technique that builds on another needs it first, as in the role-play: Greater Blade Rush needs Blade Rush, Wind Kage Bunshin needs Shadow Clone, Kirin needs Chidori and Great Fireball, and the Great Fireball needs the Sharingan, so only Uchiha learn Kirin. The grey row names the missing technique.
- **Element body flickers** need their nature: Kaze Shunshin (Wind Flicker) wind; Mizu Shunshin (Water Flicker) and Kiri Shunshin (Mist Flicker) water; Tsuchi Shunshin (Earth Flicker) earth; Hi Shunshin (Fire Flicker) fire; Rakurai Shunshin (Bolt Flicker) lightning; Suna Shunshin (Sand Flicker) sand; Yuki Shunshin (Snow Flicker) ice. One already learned in an older save stays.
- There is no Taijutsu or Ninjutsu level gate for Training Points ninja: only the class, the price and the prerequisites.
- Learning still has to fit the [ninja build](builds.html) and any tree lock (a [tree scroll](tree-scrolls.html), a bloodline, the Sharingan and so on).

## The Learn techniques window
Select a ninja and press **Learn techniques (N TP)**. The window shows the class, the TP earned and in the pool, the highest rank the class learns, and a bar to the next class. Every tree the build allows is listed, with your own techniques above World of Naruto's in each tree.
- **Learn (N TP)** buys the next rank at once.
- A grey row says why it can't be learned yet: the class is too low, a technique is needed first, the pool is short, the tree needs its scroll, the build has no room, and so on.
- Hover a row for the technique's description, chakra cost, cooldown and range.
- **Only what I can learn now** hides the grey rows; the search box filters by technique or tree name.

## Earning TP
All TP go into the one pool. The **Progression speed** setting scales the daily TP (training, sparring, classes, lessons, casting); missions, exams and epics always give their full amount.

| Source | TP |
|---|---|
| Training session (training post, battle ring, chakra apparatus, star-watching platform), once a day | 10 (15 with the sensei near) |
| Sparring | as a session, both ninja |
| Ninja classes and academy lessons (also Progression: Education's), once a day | 5 |
| Casting at enemies (within 35 cells) | 3 a cast, at most 15 a day |
| Casting otherwise | 1 per 5 casts, at most 3 a day |
| D and C missions | 5 each |
| B mission | 10 |
| A and S missions | 20 and 25 |
| Chūnin exam passed | 100 |
| Jōnin trial | 50 |
| Forbidden scroll, or an A or S ritual rank | 20 |

At the default speed a ninja who trains every day and goes on missions reaches C class in about a month, B in a year and a bit, A in about two and a half years and S in four and a half to five.

## Build caps in TP
In the ninja build, an unmastered tree holds **450 TP** for a Master, **400** for a Specialist and **350** for a Non-Specialist (a Master's extra slots 350). A mastered tree has no cap. Universal ninjutsu, taijutsu and genjutsu each have a pool: 800 (Master), 600 (Specialist), 400 (Non-Specialist), 200 with no pick. A tree that goes past its cap uses a free mastery if there is one. Your own clan or bloodline tree always counts as mastered. A respec gives back in full the TP spent on techniques the new build drops.

## Older saves
The first time a save loads with Training Points, each colony ninja gets a TP total from what they knew (the higher of the techniques' prices and the TP their best level stands for, plus 25 for each unspent technique point), all of it in the pool. Bought and scroll-taught techniques are removed so you can choose again; Academy techniques for genin and techniques that come with a bloodline stay. A letter explains it.

## NPC ninja
Village and enemy ninja buy their techniques from a TP budget by rank: genin 150 to 450, chūnin 500 to 1,400, jōnin 1,500 to 2,900, ANBU 500 to 3,000, elite 1,500 to 3,500, and kage, the Ink Master, the Grand Venator and the daimyo 5,500 or more.
