# Mounts and flying
NFRP World's creatures are ridden through **Giddy-Up**, which is recommended. Without Giddy-Up, creatures can't be ridden and the mounted arts and Fly order are unavailable; everything else works.

@@summary Riding comes from Giddy-Up. A pawn on a flying mount gets a Fly order over walls and water (25 to 60 cells), caravans with flying beasts travel faster, and the Riders tree hits harder in the saddle.

## Riding
- Giddy-Up lets a pawn ride any **tamed adult animal larger than body size 1.2** that has learned Tameness, as long as the animal can carry the rider. Most of NFRP World's big beasts qualify; the jet couatl, Ziz, the couatls and the bulettes are marked rideable on purpose.
- Workers ride to far jobs and fighters ride into battle, exactly like Giddy-Up's own animals. Giddy-Up's settings decide which animals may be ridden.
- Long-bodied mounts (the couatls) may need the rider's seat adjusted with Giddy-Up's dev offset editor.

## Flying
- A pawn riding a **flying mount** gets a **Fly** order: the pair takes off, flies over walls and water and lands still mounted.
- Range: 22 + 8 cells per point of the mount's body size, kept between **25 and 60 cells**. Both the take-off and landing cells must be unroofed. The order recharges in **900 ticks**.
- Flying mounts: Ziz, couatl, primal couatl, radiant couatl, jet couatl, hippogriff, jungle griffon, kirin, the great and elder gale hawks, and Kishinryuu (it hovers, so a pawn riding it can fly too).
- **Caravans**: when every person in a caravan has an adult flying beast of body size 1.2 or more along, world travel takes **three quarters** of the time.
- A flight in progress isn't saved: after a save and load mid-air, the rider lands on foot.

## Bajutsu (Riders: Saddle Warfare)
The Riders tree has 30 techniques from the NFRP archive's mounted-combat thread; most work only, or much better, in the saddle. See the [Riders tree page](../trees/nf-archriders.html) for every tier.
- **Saddle power**: every Riders technique hits harder while mounted, by 0.85 + 0.25 × the mount's body size, kept between **×1.0 and ×1.8**. The eight original techniques work as before on foot.
- **Riding** (passive): only in the saddle, stronger at Animals 8 and 15.
- **Fighting from the Saddle** and **Mounted Archery**: passives that switch on in the saddle.
- **Beast Bond**: the mount's creature arts appear on the rider's buttons, with a third off their cooldowns.
- **Shared Shelter**: heals and protective effects on the rider also reach the mount.
- **Aerial Perfection**: the Fly order reaches **a third farther** and recovers **twice as fast**.
- Active techniques include Fast Mount, Trample, Rearing Strike, **Unhorse** (knocks an enemy rider off), Diving Charge, Sky Watch, Ride-by Flurry, Lightning Leap from the Saddle, Strafing Pass, Crusading Steed, Second Breath, Rider's Vengeance, Skyguard Catapult, Snatch and Drop, and Overwhelming Charge.
