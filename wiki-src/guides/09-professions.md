# Professions, ANBU and gear
Venator, Bard, Soulcaster, Monster Trainer, Sensor, ANBU, Alchemist, Artisan and Ritualist.

@@summary How each profession opens: Venator and Bard by rare tree scroll, ANBU by a division title from the Kage, Monster Trainer after Binding seals, Sensor and Soulcaster with the Medical build, and Alchemist, Artisan and Ritualist on Miscellaneous slots in the build. Plus gadgets, talismans and gear.

## How each profession opens
NFRP doesn't let anyone train into a profession at will, and neither does NFRP World:
- **The Venator** (witch-hunter) and **The Art of the Bard**: a **rare tree scroll** each (see [Tree scrolls](tree-scrolls.html)). Bard techniques need a **musical instrument** in hand (flute, shamisen, war drum, horn, harp or bell staff); better instruments make stronger songs.
- **ANBU**: a **title from the Kage** (below).
- **Monster Trainer**: opens once the ninja knows **Kōsoku Fūin (Binding seals)**, NFRP's binding ritualist.
- **Sensor Specialist** and **Soulcaster**: medical forms. They need a **Medical Specialist** build, like Medical Ninjutsu; without it not even D rank can be learned.
- **Alchemist**, **Artisan** and **Ritualist**: Miscellaneous slots in the build (below).

Venator, Bard, ANBU and Monster Trainer count as universal ninjutsu in the [ninja build](builds.html).

## ANBU
- The village leader is always the **Kage** (see [Ninja ranks](ranks.html)). The Kage has an **Appoint ANBU** button.
- The Kage appoints a ninja of **chūnin rank or higher** to one ANBU division: **Falcon** (tracking and pursuit), **Owl** (intelligence), **Bear** (protection and investigation), **Cat** (infiltration), **Frog** (assassination) or **Wolf** (interrogation and truth-finding). Each ninja serves in **one** division.
- The title opens the ANBU tree, with its **ANBU arts** and **ANBU division arts**, and lets the ninja wear **that division's mask**, and only that one. Division arts need the mask worn.
- The same button revokes a title: the ANBU arts stay learned, but the mask can no longer be worn.
- In older saves, a colonist who already knew the ANBU arts and wore a mask keeps that division.
- Only pawns who know the ANBU tree can make masks.

## Alchemist, Artisan and Ritualist
In NFRP these professions take a **Miscellaneous slot**. In the Ninja build's Miscellaneous section each one **trades a mastery**, like Grand Master and Jack of Trades:
- **Take Alchemist…** picks the alchemy discipline (Brewmeister, Crystalmancy or Demolitionist) and opens the alchemy trees (Alchemy Arts, Alchemy Enhancement, Mad Science, Alchemist's Tricks). A Medical Specialist pays with a Medical mastery. Changing the discipline later still uses a Revamp Scroll. Anyone can still make the common pills and potions.
- **Take Artisan…** opens Artisan Arts and Equipment Enhancing (Artisan paths also needs its tree scroll).
- **Take Ritualist…** is taken once per ritual school: Augmentation, Deception, Restoration, Travel, Warding or Contract Bonds. The general ritual techniques need at least one school.

Ninja who already had a discipline, or already knew a technique of one of these trees or schools, keep learning it.

## Gadgets
- Spyglass, radio headset, field radio, caltrops, bell cord alarm, grappling hook, shinobi goggles, covert listening device, motion scanner and field medic kit.
- **Talismans** (one at a time, belt slot) from the Venator: Clear Mind, Warding, Hunter's and Ash Oath.
- **Sensors**: heat sensor, noise sensor and laser tripwire (which also stuns). A **desktop computer** next to a hi-tech research bench speeds up research.

## Clothing and gear
- Boots of speed, boots of striding and springing, gloves of digging, cloaks of protection, displacement and lightning, battle cloaks, fire-resistant, cold-weather and hot-weather clothing, courtier's clothing, a tactical vest and a patrol pack.
- Weapons: battle umbrellas, throwing disks, war boomerangs, senbon, and two legendary blades, the clockwork blade and the blade of the dhampir.

## Puppeteer
**Take Puppeteer…** in the ninja build (Miscellaneous, trades a mastery) opens the tree **Kugutsu no Jutsu (Puppet Technique)**. Puppets are built objects: a Puppeteer makes them at the **puppet workshop** (research: puppet making), carries them folded in the pack, and unseals them in a fight.

**On the threads.** An unsealed puppet stands beside its Puppeteer and is ordered like a drafted fighter. It must stay within 12 cells: farther than that, or if the Puppeteer is downed, stunned or gone, the threads snap and the puppet folds up where it stands. **Seal puppet** folds it on purpose; within 2 cells it goes back into the pack. A puppet keeps its damage until repaired. At 0 it leaves a **broken puppet**, which the workshop rebuilds for a third of the materials.

| Puppet | Role | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Karasu (Crow)** | Fast attacker, fragile | Poisoned arm blades; **Poison Needles** (three needles, 10 cells); **Poison Smoke Bomb** (smoke, and everyone of flesh within 3 cells is poisoned, friend or foe) |
| **Kuroari (Black Ant)** | Trap | Saw-blade arms; **Capture** pulls a foe within 3 cells into its barrel: helpless until released or until the puppet breaks, folds or loses its threads |
| **Sanshōuo (Salamander)** | Shield, slow and very tough | Tail strike; the shield frill (below) |

**The tree:**
- **Chakra Threads** (D): the basis.
- **Puppet Technique** (C): unseal the next puppet you carry. Also needed to build puppets.
- **Two-Puppet Control** (B) and **Three-Puppet Control** (A): two, then three puppets at once.
- **Puppet Repair** (C): mends the worst damage of a puppet within 3 cells; within 4 cells of a puppet workshop, everything.
- **Black Secret Technique: Salamander** (B): the frill opens for 20 seconds; shots and techniques aimed from range at an ally within 3 cells behind the Salamander hit the puppet instead.
- **Black Secret Technique: Machine One Shot** (A): with a foe caught in Black Ant and Crow within 3 cells of it, Crow's blades go through the barrel. Very heavy damage.

Puppet poison slows and weakens; it wears off in about two days and does not kill. Puppets themselves can't be poisoned.
