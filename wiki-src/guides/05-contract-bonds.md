# Contract bonds
Two colonists bound soul to soul, with techniques that only work between them.

@@summary Two of your people bound for life with one ritual. The bond gives techniques that only work between them (chakra gifts, pain switch, shields, healing, sacrifices) and passives that trigger when the partner falls.

## The ritual
- Learn **Ritual of the Contract Bonds** (Contract bonds tree, D) and target another of your own people. Each person can have one bond, for life.

## Techniques
- **Mind Link**: both feel better for a day, at any distance.
- **Chakra Gift** (D to S): pour 10% to 50% of your maximum chakra into the partner.
- **Protective Shove**: knock the partner out of harm's way and shake them free of stuns.
- **Pain Switch**: take all of the partner's pain for an hour.
- **Bond Shield**: the partner takes half damage for a short while.
- **Wounded Balance**: heal the partner's wounds and take half of them yourself.
- **Final Parting** and **Ultimate Sacrifice**: give your life to heal or even bring back the partner.

## Passives
- **Distress Call**: feel it when the partner falls. **Bond Fury**: fight harder when they do. **Just a Little Longer**: a downed partner barely bleeds. **Don't Leave Me**: survive the sacrifices, downed instead of dead.
