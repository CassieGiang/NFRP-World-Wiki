# Update notes
What changed in recent updates, newest first.

## Update 151: mission bonding
- Squads come home closer: shared mission memories, "fought side by side", "patched me up on the road", and sometimes a grudge against the leader of a failed mission.
- Three missions together make two ninja **comrades**; a comrade's death hits harder.
- A ninja back from a solo mission is proud of it.
- A trip skipped to the end keeps its planned times in the log.
- Setting: Living world → Mission bonding.

## Update 150: visual and combat fixes

- Signature art (phoenix, dragons, sharks, coffins, Tree Tomb and the rest) now keeps its own painted colours instead of being recoloured by the element tint, and Suzaku no longer shows a beast head over its phoenix. Tombs, coffins and the monolith play larger.
- Combos no longer cut their own techniques short.
- Passive perks (blade basics and similar) no longer pop up as text or count as ailments, and specialisations no longer show the genjutsu swirl.
- Stuns from water, moon and sword techniques show the neutral swirl instead of lightning crackle.
- Leafless winter trees show their bare art again.
- Every NFRP window can be dragged and opens where you last left it.

## Update 149: polish
- **Ninja build window:** the planner's rows, level buttons and rank strips line up, notes wrap instead of being cut off, the rank legend is a full line, and the frame fits its content.
- **Technique palette:** techniques World of Naruto shows only while drafted (taijutsu such as Kick and Combo) are now listed greyed with "draft to use", so their tab no longer disappears.
- **Legendary blade trees** start closed and open the first time a pawn equips that blade, so new pawns no longer start with arts for blades they don't have. Techniques already learned are kept.
- **Technique previews** (the small looping effect when hovering a technique button) are now off by default; turn them on under Looks. Configs that had them on from the old default are switched off once.
- **Guide:** the first-time guide points to the new settings tabs.
- **Icons:** buttons that borrowed a vanilla icon missing in this game version (the Sensei button showed a red square) now use NFRP icons.

## Update 148: interface redesign
- **Ninja orders:** NFRP's buttons on a selected ninja (Combo, Kit, Sidearm, throwing tools, Formation, Fall back, Guard post, Stance) are grouped into one **Ninja orders** button that opens a ring at the mouse. Stance and Mark as priority stay on the bar. A setting brings the separate buttons back. See [Stances and squads](stances-and-squads.html).
- **Settings:** tabs down the side (Looks, Combat, World, Village life, Performance, Dev) with setting counts, "changed from default" counts, a search box over every tab, a look-preset menu, and Reset this tab / Reset everything. See [Getting started](getting-started.html#the-settings-window).
- **Shinobi tab:** a new pawn tab gathers rank and level, nickname, fame and infamy, stance, fall-back point, kit and sidearm, team sync, the break meter, rivals and grudges, deeds and village relics.
- **Interrogation panel:** an Interrogation button on prisoners shows resistance, today's odds, mood, the questioning mode and what they have revealed, with Raid it for camps.
- **First-time guide:** a contents list with checkmarks and new pages on break and combos, stances and squads, ninja tools, fame and rivals, missions, the living world and storytellers.
- The combo builder, kit editor, Bingo Book, live squad mission window and mission records share one dark look.

## Update 146: new Ninja world and build windows
- The Ninja world window is split into six tabs: Overview, Villages & diplomacy, Bingo Book, Missions, News and Research. The Overview shows your village, squads away with progress bars and Watch buttons, what's coming up, your ninja with their stance and status, and the latest news.
- The ninja build window is now a one-page planner: a None / Non-Specialist / Specialist / Master choice per kind, a D-to-S rank strip for each (green reachable, gold with a mastery, grey out of reach), slot counts, universal caps and an "If you confirm" box. The build rules are unchanged.

## Update 145: ninja backstories give chakra
- The 33 ninja-trained backstories now give World of Naruto's Chakra trait (only when World of Naruto is loaded). Civilian backstories never do, and a pawn never gets the trait twice.

## Update 144: ninja tool kit
- **Ninja pouches** (cloth, leather, sealing scroll) and **kits**: choose what each ninja carries; idle pawns refill from storage. See [Ninja tool kit](ninja-tool-kit.html).
- **Stances use tools** with no chakra: smoke and kunai openers, senbon from hiding, bombs at groups (never next to allies), nets, pills for allies.
- **Thrown blades come back**: they land on the ground or stick in the target and fall out later; a small chance to break on a hit. Bombs and pills are used up.
- **Sidearm swap by range**, standing down when a weapon-swap mod is loaded. **Enemy ninja carry kits** by rank.
- **Guard posts**: draw a hold-fire area as well as the ring. **Formations**: places behind a wall move to a cell the leader can see and reach.

## Updates 142 and 143: wounded retreat and the Barrage stance
- Badly hurt pawns fall back to a bed or to cover behind their friends and return once they've healed a little. Each colonist has a **Fall back** button (Off, Cautious, Normal, Last stand); stances shift the point. Hurt enemies run for the map edge and may come back as rivals.
- New stance **Barrage**: a ranged ninjutsu caster that keeps its distance, casts as often as chakra allows and holds back big area techniques near allies. See [Stances and squads](stances-and-squads.html).

## Update 141: stances and squads
- Drafted ninja get combat stances: Vanguard, Support, Assassin and Sealer change which techniques they use and how they move. Assassins earn an ambush bonus while unseen.
- Mark an enemy as the squad's priority target (right-click it), march drafted groups in formation, and post guards on patrol routes with hold fire.
- Medical ninja heal or carry downed allies on their own when it is safe. See [Stances and squads](stances-and-squads.html).

## Update 140: live squad missions
- Squad missions are planned from what your ninja can do (rank, elements, taijutsu, medics, sensors, genjutsu, summons) and can be watched in a live window with a map of the road and a log.
- What happens on the road comes home with them: wounds, loot, bounty shares, relics, hidden camps, fame, hunger and tiredness.
- Village mission rank: harder missions open as your village completes missions (B at level 2, A at 3, S at 4).
- Ten relics with small permanent bonuses for the whole village, and a Mission records window.
- New settings in the Living world section. See *Live squad missions*.

## Updates 133, 135, 137 and 139: the living world
- **The world moves on its own:** villages besiege, capture, raze and found settlements, sign ceasefires and go to war; named ninja of every village rank up, marry, have children and die; it all goes into the new **World news** in the Ninja world panel. See [The living world](living-world.html).
- **Squad missions:** send 1 to 4 ninja on a D- to S-rank mission off the map; they come back days later with a report.
- **Ninja society:** village leaders with attitudes and successions, genin teams, a chūnin exam host that rotates every year with named genin, envoys, hostages, tribute and marriage alliances, clans in your colony with heads, feuds and requests, and villages that remember what you did.
- **The living land:** travellers on the world map to trade with, escort, ambush or recruit; landmarks to explore; three legendary beasts (the Ashmaw, the Tidewyrm and the Thunderhorn) to hunt or befriend; pilgrims, monks, students and refugees at your door; a market that follows the wars.
- **Small touches:** festivals in other villages you can send a delegation to, letters from the ninja you know, and the stories of their deaths.
- Every part has its own switch under Mod settings > NFRP World > World and events > Living world. The legendary beasts borrow other creatures' art until their own is painted.

## Updates 134, 136 and 138: animation upgrade
- **Effects drawn in code:** shockwave rings, impact bursts, cones, beams, speed lines, ripples, lightning, slashes, domes, wind spirals, dust, sparks and smoke are now drawn in code: smooth every frame, sharp at any zoom, in the element's colours. Signature art stays painted. A new setting, **Procedural effects** (Full, Simple, Off), brings the painted animations back if you prefer them. See [Visual effects](visual-effects.html).
- **Impact feel:** a short pause on the heaviest hits and on breaks, dark (colour-inverting) flashes for shadow, ink and genjutsu, heat haze over fire and big impacts, debris that bounces and skids, and fading copies of a ninja along dashes.
- **The body:** throws, palms, slams, draws and grabs move the whole body, and the hands pull back before a strike. Kunai and senbon stay stuck where they hit; stone skin, frost, ink and seal tags show on the body.
- **Barriers and shots:** hostile shots slow down outside NFRP barriers, and wind barriers bend about half of them away (a gameplay setting, on by default).
- New settings for each part under Mod settings > NFRP World.

## Update 132: storytellers
- **Three ninja storytellers:** Hisame the Chronicler (like Cassandra), Old Sensei Genkō (like Phoebe) and Kitsunebi the Trickster (like Randy). See [Storytellers](storytellers.html).
- Your village's renown sets the pace; generic early threats (mech clusters, manhunters, infestations, ship parts) are mostly replaced by ninja ones.
- A ninja calendar of festivals, exams, mission offers in quiet seasons and village war after the truce.
- Story arcs over weeks: returning rivals and their camps, bounty hunters, deserters and the hunter-nin who follow them (or spies), rogue ninja, summon beasts gone wild, Black Ink smugglers, wandering masters.
- Their portraits are placeholders for now; painted ones are coming.

## Update 131: plants, items and signature art
- **Plants:** 47 more plants repainted in the flatter base-game style, and every NFRP plant now grows in two looks. Belan, golden beech, ironwood, silverbark and zalantar trees lose their leaves in winter.
- **Items:** about 530 item pictures repainted: weapons, armour and clothing, resources, gems, potions, bombs, books, scrolls and gear.
- **Status looks:** clearer status rings around affected pawns and new symbols over their heads.
- **Technique walls:** escarpment, glacier, briar and hallucinatory walls now join up with each other and with ordinary walls.
- **Summon circles:** each element has its own painted summoning circle.
- **Signature art:** 33 techniques that used generic effects now show their own beast or object, such as the rainbow dragon, phoenix, thunder dragon, true hydra, megamouth shark, crescent dragons, tree tomb, sand and earth coffins, iron sand monolith, meteors, stalagmite forest and the iron demon. Drawing only: damage and timing are unchanged.
- Six effects (earth spikes, seal circle, scroll unroll, flame pillar, burrow eruption, shell guard) and the storm cloud were redrawn without cut edges.

## Updates 128 to 130: reputation, duels and intel
- **Witnesses:** deeds only become fame or infamy if someone who isn't one of yours lives to tell them; stories seen only by your prisoners wait until one goes free. See [Reputation, duels and intel](reputation-duels-and-intel.html).
- **Masked names:** a colonist in a masked-guard mask earns fame under the mask's name, with its own Bingo Book page; unmasked in front of outsiders, the names become one.
- **Infamy:** executions, harvesting captives, killing the helpless, betraying guests and allies earn a darker reputation: dark nicknames, more bounty hunters, less goodwill with the villages, harder intimidation, less inspiration.
- **Village renown:** your ninja's fame makes your village known: wanderers join, missions pay more, traders give a discount, raids grow a little. Shown in the Ninja world panel.
- **Duel challenges:** famous colonists get formal challenges (rivals too). Accept, and nobody else may step in.
- **Teachers and legacy:** famous sparring partners teach more; fallen legends leave the village their will and some renown; bury them, or thieves may come for the body.
- **Tales:** famous deeds become tales for your artists.
- **Body and bloodline bounties:** dead-only bounties, handing in bodies for the bounty, a legend's bloodline, clan ninja as prime targets.
- **Intel, spying and interrogation:** the Bingo Book fills in as you learn more; spy missions; interrogate prisoners for their secrets, camps you can raid and raids being planned.
- **Beheading a village:** kill or capture a village's leader and it is leaderless (and weaker) for a season.
- New settings for each part under Combat & AI.

## Updates 124 to 127: fame, the Bingo Book and rivals
- **Fame:** ninja, yours and the enemy's, build fame from kills, breaks, combos, won fights, missions and promotions. Known, Feared and Legendary ninja inspire allies and intimidate enemies near them in a fight, which also changes how fast break meters fill. See [Fame, the Bingo Book and rivals](fame-bingo-book-and-rivals.html).
- **Nicknames:** famous ninja earn a nickname from what they are known for, grander as they get more famous. A nickname you set yourself is never replaced.
- **Bingo Book:** a new button in the Ninja world panel. Famous ninja and their bounties; bounty hunters come for your most famous ninja, and you can hunt famous enemies for their bounty.
- **Rivals:** enemies who get away can come back for the ninja who hurt them, stronger, scarred and harder to beat the same way twice, leading a raid or alone for a duel.
- **New wing frames** for the 12 flying creatures, matching their new look.
- New settings under Combat & AI: **Fame**, **Inspire and intimidate strength**, **Weak enemies may flee**, **Earned nicknames**, **Bingo Book hunters**, **Rivals**.

## Updates 118 to 123: break and combos, names and backstories
- **Break and combos:** steady pressure fills a break meter under a fighter's name; a broken fighter can't act for a few seconds and a nearby ninja runs a combo you set with the new **Combo** button. Combos grow from 2 to 5 actions with level. Enemy ninja do it too. Settings: **Break and combos** and **Break speed**. See [Break and combos](break-and-combos.html).
- **Ninja names:** ninja, villagers and World of Naruto's ninja get Japanese-style names, some with nicknames. See [Ninja names and backstories](names-and-backstories.html).
- **Ninja backstories:** 42 childhoods and 53 adulthoods from the ninja world for every NFRP and World of Naruto ninja faction.
- New settings, off by default: **Ninja names for new colonists** and **Ninja backstories for new colonists**, for starts other than the ninja ones.
- **New creature art:** 95 more creatures and summons repainted in the flatter base-game style. Flying creatures still flap with their old wings until their new wing frames arrive.
- Giddy-Up is listed as recommended, not required.

## Updates 106 to 117: ninja research
- **Ninja research:** 19 projects in four tiers on World of Naruto's ninja research tab. Starter gear stays free; C to S-rank seals, alchemy, gadgets, metallurgy, gems, bloodline work, legendary weapons and relics are researched. See [Ninja research](ninja-research.html).
- The scroll library, chakra study apparatus and desktop computer now link to research benches and speed up research; the highest projects need one linked.
- A **Ninja research** button at the top of the Ninja world panel opens the research screen on the ninja tab and shows your progress.
- New settings: **Ninja research cost** and **Start with ninja research done**. Saves from before the tree get the projects they already use, once, with a letter.
- Every item on the [Items](../items.html) page lists the research it needs.
- NFRP World works with only the base game, Harmony, Taran Magic Framework and World of Naruto. Giddy-Up and Ferny's Progression mods are optional. See [Recommended mods](recommended-mods.html).

## Updates 106 to 117: looks and fixes
- **Lasting effects form and fade:** storm zones bloom and fade, barriers rise and crumble, status auras fade in and out, ranged casts send a visible carrier, summons arrive in a seal circle. New setting of the same name. See [Visual effects](visual-effects.html#lasting-effects-form-and-fade).
- **Stuns** from NFRP techniques show a swirl in the technique's element instead of electric crackle.
- **Storm Cloud** gets a full presentation: launched from the mouth, drifting layers, crackle, drizzle and strikes from the cloud.
- The six ghostly creatures (apparition, nightwraith banshee, lacedon, mist wrath wolf, swamp jellyfish and jungle griffon) are drawn see-through.
- New creature art in a flatter base-game style for the cave bear, winter wolf, ghoul, giant rabbit, Halbeisen warhorse and gale hawk; the rest follow in coming updates.
- Technique tree backgrounds are repainted in a darker NFRP look.
- Technique sounds fall back to base-game sounds when a DLC is missing.
- Things created by techniques (walls, domes, clouds and the like) have info cards.
- Disarm and other hostile single-target techniques never hit their own caster.
- ANBU masks sit correctly on the head from every side. Seijaku no Maru (Silence Dome) uses the shadow dome.
- Eight weapons and two brews use base-game style lowercase names.
- The game starts faster: NFRP World's alchemy setup no longer slows loading.

## Update 105: buildings in the base-game style
- Every NFRP building and piece of furniture is repainted to sit beside base-game and World of Naruto furniture: seen from above, square to the grid, flat colours with a dark outline.
- Each one fits its footprint, so nothing pokes past its selection corners. The scribe desk and alchemy table turn properly when rotated.

## Update 104: Ninja world icon
- The ninja world button on the bottom bar has its own icon.

## Update 102: soft effect edges
- Burst animations, beast heads and status marks fade at their edges instead of ending in a hard straight line.

## Update 100: effects that match the name
- A technique's colours and particles now follow its name: purple lightning for Shidensen, pink petals for Sakura Tsuisutā, red fire for Crimson Fireball, black ink, silver crescents and more. More than 450 techniques changed look. New setting: **Effects match technique names**. See [Visual effects](visual-effects.html#effects-that-match-the-name).

## Updates 97 to 103: fixes
- Four hidden villages that were sometimes never added to a world are now added, in new and existing saves.
- Dash kicks such as Capoeira's Aú play their move.
- Your own casts stick to their target instead of being dropped for an auto-attack.
- A shot that can't fire because chakra ran out during the wind-up now shows "Fizzled".
- Enemy ninja judge each technique's real reach, never throw an exploding technique where they'd stand in its own blast, step back from melee fighters with ranged techniques, and join a pincer when a friend is already fighting the target.
- Shots from technique bows and guns that fire plain projectiles get a muzzle flash.
- Fast projectiles flying straight at each other clash instead of passing through.
- A Kishinryuu rider who lands is put back in the saddle.
- Saving while a bonded partner was gone no longer writes a broken reference.
- The ANBU mask from **Don the Mask** shows its side profile when the ninja faces sideways, and isn't drawn twice over a worn ANBU mask. Stat bonuses to hit chance and accuracy show as skill levels (+5), not as a percentage.

## Update 96: signature looks, defensive moments and a new settings window
- About 35 S-rank techniques have a signature look of their own: a seal circle that flashes once per hand seal, a release with its own shapes, and an aftermath. See [Visual effects](visual-effects.html#signature-looks).
- **Defensive moments:** parry and block sparks, dodge side-steps with afterimages, the full substitution sequence, and ripples when a barrier is hit.
- The settings window has tabs, a tooltip on every setting, look presets (Cinematic, Balanced, Performance, Vanilla-like) and a first-time guide. Your settings keep their values. See [Getting started](getting-started.html#the-settings-window).

## Update 95: enemy tactics and performance
- Enemy ninja use their techniques with tactics: the best technique for the chakra, stuns first, no area techniques on their own side, escapes when nearly beaten, and on Smart keeping distance, focus fire, pincers, support and medics. New setting: **Enemy ninja tactics** (Off, Basic, Smart; default Smart).
- **Effects quality** (Full, Balanced, Performance) and **Auto-reduce in big fights**.
- Medieval Overhaul: NFRP World's woods are no longer turned into Medieval Overhaul timber.
- Long sessions stay smooth: effect timing no longer drifts in long games, and old effects are cleaned up.

## Update 94: fizzles, finishing blows and a balance pass
- **Keep casts going** and **Show fizzled casts**: a cast is no longer quietly cancelled; if it ends before the technique fires, you see a grey puff and "Fizzled".
- **Finishing blow moments**, **Ambient ninja life** and **Weather interplay**. See [Visual effects](visual-effects.html#finishing-blows-life-and-weather).
- Balance: 126 status, buff and blink techniques no longer deal a stray 10 damage (allies-only buffs used to hurt your own team). 30 techniques that were far too strong for their chakra cost more and hit a little less (Five Sharks, Water Gun, Typhoon, Finger Bullets, Rail Gun and others), and 18 weak ones (mostly stuns and paralysis) cost less. The Kazeraikou arts and the Eight Gates are unchanged.
- Enemy jōnin are now clearly stronger than chūnin.
- The chūnin exam needs ninja level 6 to enter. New setting: **Progression speed** (25% to 200%). See [Ranks](ranks.html).
- Fixes from the game log: animal trainability, food rot, smelting and butchering of NFRP items, the sealed ruin quest, and several pack and gear textures.

## Updates 84 to 92: stability and weapon arts
- Fixed three crashes during long fights and channelled casts, and a slow memory leak from effects.
- **Weapon art choreography**: weapon techniques draw, dash, flurry, rise and thrust with the weapon. See [Visual effects](visual-effects.html#weapon-arts).
- Belts, rigs and vests drawn on the body no longer show a red X, and dropped items are smaller on the ground.
- Casts at a target someone else has reserved go ahead.

## Update 83: polish everywhere
- Every remaining soft particle is crisp now: embers, flames, leaves, petals, sparks, drops, rings, swirls, auras, lightning bolts, gusts, healing and seal marks.
- Constructs and bursts grow slightly past full size and settle.
- Techniques that had no wind-up get a short one (a draw-back and point for shots, gathering for support techniques), and the hands lower after every cast. Cast times are unchanged.
- Shadows on the ground under flying beasts, floating hands, thrown and launched bodies, projectiles and debris.
- Area warnings: a marker fills on the ground while an area technique winds up; enemy ones have a reddish edge.
- Hits flash the target white, throw sparks away from the attacker, and heavy hits scatter debris.
- Kunai and senbon fly point-first with a slight wobble and a short trail.
- Flicker techniques leave puffs and afterimages, leaps raise dust, water walking leaves ripples, climbers shake leaves loose, and the substitution log drops with a bounce.
- Summons land with a squash; Kishinryuu arrives in a storm puff.
- Burning, frozen, poisoned, bound, genjutsu, stunned, bleeding and chakra-exhausted ninja show it on the body.
- See [Visual effects](visual-effects.html).

## Update 82: constructs
About 110 techniques now build something visible in their own material: elemental beasts that fly at the target, giant hands, walls and domes that rise and crumble, prisons, spikes, binds and body coatings. See [Visual effects](visual-effects.html#constructs).

## Update 81: iron sand, teams and callouts
- Iron sand techniques of the Masamune clan show giant sand hands on magnetic tethers, sand walls, orbiting orbs, spires, a grinding prism, needle rain and rail slugs.
- Two allies hitting one foe from opposite sides land a joint pincer strike.
- Elemental combos show as one big effect the size of the combo.
- XP pop-ups over your ninja, and technique-name callouts when ninja cast.

## Update 80: channels and hand signs
- All animated effects are repainted in the crisp style.
- Long casts are channelled with orbiting motes and a weapon pose (planted, raised, upright, level or pointed), ending with a swing and a shoulder rest.
- Hand signs now snap through clearly different signs, a fixed sequence per technique.
- New: **one-handed signs**, learned only through training. See [Hand signs](hand-signs.html).

## Update 79: taijutsu
Close-range techniques dash in, land punch strings, throw foes over the head or launch them into the air.

## Update 78: Storm Dragon stream
Kazeraikou's **Arashi Ryuu: Storm Dragon** pours out of the cloud as a flowing stream with a dragon head leading it and two smaller escorts. Kazeraikou is by Marii from NFRP.

## Update 77: crisp effects and mouth casting
- Techniques fly as crisp crescents and spears, waves sweep as walls, beams charge and hold, hits burst and leave marks on the ground.
- Glow is capped so overlapping effects don't blow out to white, and each use shows fewer, bigger particles.
- Breath, spit and fireball techniques come out of the mouth, with a hand raised to the lips.

## Update 76: gear on the body
- The bandolier, chest rig, load vest and equipment belt are drawn fitted to the body for every body shape, like vanilla's bandolier.
- Bags, quivers and sling pouches no longer show loose straps; the messenger bag, duffle, field backpack, medical pack and three sling pouches were repainted without straps.

## Update 75: Storm Dragon
- The storm dragon flies at half the speed so you can follow it (looks only).
- Quivers and packs lost the loose strap loops that stuck out beside the body.
