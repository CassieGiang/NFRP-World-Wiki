## Updates 308–313: every technique its own shape
**Art**
- **Shapes from the technique:** the newer painted pieces no longer all look like a round burst. Each one takes its shape from what the technique does: fire and sound breaths fan out toward the target, thrusts and shots are long streaks, blade techniques leave crossing slash marks, earth and ice throw spikes up from the ground, binds are tangles, mind techniques warp the air, smoke and mist roll along the ground, and heals send motes upward. Aimed pieces point at the target.
- **Auras rise:** self buffs show an aura rising from the ground seen from the side, in one of several shapes: a crown of flames, a twisting column, an upward spiral or a loose drift, each in its element's look.
- The older round pieces are being repainted in the new shapes in stages.

**Hand-made animations**
- **The freeze:** a hand-made animation's hit always gets its short freeze, even right after another hit, and a shorter one in big fights.
- **Readable on any ground:** dark pieces get a soft pale halo and light pieces a soft shadow, so they show on dark soil, at night and on snow.

## Updates 302–307: bringing every technique to the level of the best
**Art**
- **Hand-made animations show only their own art.** No hand-made technique animation draws a shared stock picture any more; where a piece of its own is still being painted, it shows its drawn effect instead. Thirty of their own pieces that were painted pointing sideways now point along the cast, and the pieces of the newer groups are drawn at their full size.
- **Technique families get their own painted set:** every family of techniques without a hand-made animation shows its own painted gathering at the hands and its own aftermath where it lands, once installed.
- **Painted flights for shots:** every technique shot that still flew as a plain picture gets a painted flight of its own, once installed (the shark blast and clay bombs already fly as the painted shark and clay bird).
- More B- and C-rank techniques got their own painted piece where they hit.

## Updates 290–301: every technique gets its own painted art, and many fixes
**Art**
- **Every technique gets its own painted effect.** S- and A-rank techniques now show their own painted piece where they hit (239 techniques). B-, C- and D-rank techniques follow in stages (562). Plain shots get a piece where they land, and self buffs one on the user (170). A piece shows only once it is installed; until then the technique looks as before.
- **Living domes:** barrier domes breathe, ripple where a shot hits them, and pop and shrink when they end.

**Fixes**
- After loading an earlier save, small effects, creature flashes, camera shake and finisher cinematics no longer stay off for a while, and nothing from the later save is left on screen.
- Prisoners no longer heal themselves with seals from their pockets (they still use them when breaking out).
- Enemy ninja keep using Storm Clone, Kenseijin stances, storm clouds and golems in normal fights.
- Goodwill changes from NFRP name their reason (ninja mission, hidden village ties, ninja world, festival).
- A ninja who falls on a squad mission is remembered properly after loading; signature techniques of enemies who died are freed a day later.
- Formations face the right way when the leader faces east or west; youth seals leave children alone; coatings stay on both halves of a split stack.

## Updates 263–289: signature techniques, our own art everywhere, a shinobi furniture style and smarter storytellers
**Signature techniques** (see [signature techniques](signature-techniques.html))
- Your ninja design techniques of their own from 24 shapes, 34 modifiers and 22 painted motifs, named in Japanese and English, and develop them at a training spot.
- Parts are earned through missions, rivals, fame, old seals and training. Missions, duels, fame, sealed caches and training also bring inspirations: three ideas to choose from.
- Scrolls teach a signature to another of your ninja. Copying techniques can't take one.
- Enemy leaders, jōnin and rivals carry signatures. You learn them once you see them used; they show in the Bingo Book.
- A **Signature techniques** window on the bottom bar lists your techniques, the parts and known enemy techniques.

**Storytellers** (see [storytellers](storytellers.html))
- Squad requests from friendly villages that pay extra when answered, moments of insight that inspire a ninja, rivals who come back with a signature, and ninja tools and shinobi weapons in the smuggler's stash.
- The small portraits of Hisame and Kitsunebi are recropped to show the whole head.

**Art**
- **Our own art for hand-made animations:** the Kazeraikou storm, burial, eye, palm, hound, falcon and bear groups, the star, shadow, swarm, bone, Akimichi and masked guard groups, and the six prototype heroes (iron sand, folding space, seal blink, clockwork and fang blades) now draw their own painted pieces instead of shared ones.
- **More signature looks:** dragons, birds, hounds and tigers in more elements, ten new pale motifs (serpent, crane, wolf, comet, turtle shell, spider web, lotus, mirror shards, crescent moon, sun flare).
- **Shinobi furniture style** (Ideology): our own look for 34 vanilla furniture pieces, benches, lamps and the campfire, drawn from above; long benches are drawn long.
- **Slate tab buttons** under a selected pawn or thing (also with RimHUD). Setting: NFRP tab buttons.

**Gear and crafting**
- **24 shinobi weapons**: classic blades, chain and rope weapons, polearms, parrying weapons and throwing tools (see [shinobi weapons](shinobi-weapons.html)).
- **Inscribe seal** at the scribe desk opens a window listing the colony's weapons and apparel, on the map or worn.

**Fixes**
- The Ninja world panel says "Festival: in 6 days" instead of "around in 6 days", and ninja away from home show "when back home" for their next exam instead of "ready".
- Demanding tribute names the right renown tier (Renowned), and the leaders' summit shows a sworn village as sworn instead of neutral.

## Updates 174–262: cleaner hand-made animations, painted creatures, jutsu chains and fixes
**Hand-made animations**
- **Clean casts:** a technique with its own hand-made animation now shows only its own art. During the wind-up the hands form the seals, with no shared seal circle or chakra gather; there is no generic flash, ring or start burst at the cast.
- **Shooting techniques** (Great Fireball, Chakra Needles, the storm shots and others) start their animation when the shot is fired and aim it at the real target. Before, in normal play it could play early at the caster, before the shot left.
- **Great Fireball** is redrawn: flame gathers at the mouth while the ball swells, a large churning fireball flies with the real shot and lands as it hits, then a sharp flash and a spreading dome of fire. No smoke cloud covers the dome.
- **Great Fire Annihilation** pours a painted sea of fire from the mouth that reaches past the target, and no longer ends in dark smoke.
- **Painted creatures fly their own art** from caster to target: water and bug dragons, fire dragon, ice tiger, fire sparrow, clay birds, water sharks, smoke dragon, wind tiger, the C2 dragon, lightning dragon and hound; the earth dragon, wood dragon, shadow beast, earth hounds and sand hands rise from the ground.
- **Painted art stays visible in long sessions:** after many effects had played, some painted art (fireballs, creatures) became almost invisible for the rest of the game. Fixed.
- **Effects face the right way:** flares, pillars, splashes and spikes stand upright; breaths, needle volleys and thrusts point at the target.
- **Weather techniques** bring their rain and wind within seconds; Ryūraisen's dragon is red; riders step off their mount for a leap and back on after; Mega Palm Thrust and Partial Expansion swell the user's own flesh arm; the Bug Dragon flies slower and larger so it reads; Storm Bullets fly slower; Double-Headed Wolf merges ninja and dog into one painted wolf; Crescent Dragons' scrolls circle the user; Starfall, Black Drizzle and Stalagmite Forest last as long as the technique; storm clouds show their reach.
- **Element combos** (firestorm, blizzard and the rest) play their own painted art.
- **Barrier rings:** all 14 materials have their own painted wall (water, sand, bugs, paper, crystal, ink and more); Shark Wall has three sharks circling it.
- **Cleaner effects:** heat shimmer only on fire; rings and shadow ribbons are one continuous piece; Silence Dome has its own look; standing auras dim while their pawn's technique plays; shared impact stars and smoke stay out where a technique has its own art; 29 cut-off effect animations re-cut whole.

**Combat and progression**
- **Jutsu chains** and **solo combos** for trained ninja, with a chain tab in the build window and a letter when a chain step, a combo slot or a solo combo unlocks.
- **Projectile targeting** per technique: some home in on a moving target, some can be aimed at the ground, the rest fly straight.
- **Kenseijin stances** give more dodge, a blade in hand turns some melee swings aside, and stuns wear off faster.
- **World of Naruto duplicates:** where both mods had the same technique (Kawarimi, element duplicates, the Uchiha and Hyūga ones, the Eight Gates), NFRP's version replaces it; pawns who knew World of Naruto's version are taught NFRP's.
- **Scroll chakra bar:** chakra is an ink stroke on a paper scroll, shows the aimed technique's cost and what is left after it, and turns vermilion when low. Setting: Looks → Information → Scroll chakra bar.

**Fixes**
- A new-world crash from the three NFRP ideology roles having no names is fixed.
- Makibishi caltrops no longer log an error when thrown.

## Updates 152–173: hand-made animations for every technique, and fixes
- **Hand-made hero animations:** 227 techniques now have their own hand-choreographed animation, with a wind-up, launch, travel, impact with a short pause, aftermath and a clean fade. They cover all of Kazeraikou and a set of signature techniques, plus the nine major clans (Uchiha, Hyūga, Aburame, Inuzuka, Kaguya, Nara, Akimichi, Yamanaka, Hoshigaki), the ANBU, Red Leg, Busosen and Kenseijin. Each one plays the mod's own painted effects at its key moments: the dragons, phoenix, sharks, hydra and other beasts fly their own art. Setting: Looks → Hero animations.
- **Every other technique** (about 1,300) plays a hero-style animation from its family (waves, streams, binds, blows, auras, barriers and more), sized to its real range and coloured by its element. Setting: Looks → Hero-style family animations.
- **Melee techniques** step the user in to the target for the hit, then back.
- **When the user becomes the technique,** the body vanishes into it: Piercing Fang's spinning drill (the dog drills alongside from Double Piercing Fang on, and each enemy is hit as the drill reaches them), the shooting star, the charging bear.
- **Inuzuka techniques** are performed with the bonded dog: it sniffs, howls, bites, mirrors the beast stance and answers the pack call.
- **Three-sword style** now needs a sword (polearms such as the naginata no longer count). The fighter holds a second sword in the other hand and a third in the teeth. Setting: Looks → Three-sword style look.
- **Silent Kill:** an enemy below half health dies outright; one caught unaware is struck in the neck or heart.
- **Smoke Escape:** the user is unseen for a moment while the smoke is thick.
- **Chakra:** pawns who know no techniques no longer count as "out of chakra", which had made breaking them and the Barrage stance behave oddly.
- **Creatures:** 16 creatures redrawn in the flat style (wrath wolves, hags, Lacedon, Blizzard Bear and others).
- **Descriptions:** 41 S-rank techniques no longer say their rank twice.

## Update 146: new Ninja world and build windows
- The Ninja world window is split into six tabs: Overview, Villages & diplomacy, Bingo Book, Missions, News and Research. The Overview shows your village, squads away with progress bars and Watch buttons, what's coming up, your ninja with their stance and status, and the latest news.

## Update 145: ninja backstories give chakra
- The 33 ninja-trained backstories now give World of Naruto's Chakra trait (only when World of Naruto is loaded). Civilian backstories never do, and a pawn never gets the trait twice.

## Update 144: ninja tool kit
- **Ninja pouches** (cloth, leather, sealing scroll) and **kits**: choose what each ninja carries; idle pawns refill from storage. See [Ninja tool kit](ninja-tool-kit.html).
- **Stances use tools** with no chakra: smoke and kunai openers, senbon from hiding, bombs at groups (never next to allies), nets, pills for allies.
- **Thrown blades come back**: they land on the ground or stick in the target and fall out later; a small chance to break on a hit. Bombs and pills are used up.
- **Sidearm swap by range**, standing down when a weapon-swap mod is loaded. **Enemy ninja carry kits** by rank.
- **Guard posts**: draw a hold-fire area as well as the ring. **Formations**: places behind a wall move to a cell the leader can see and reach.

## Updates 142 and 143: wounded retreat and the Barrage stance
- Badly hurt pawns fall back to a bed or to cover behind their friends and return once they've healed a little. Each colonist has a **Fall back** button (Off, Cautious, Normal, Last stand); stances shift the point. Hurt enemies run for the map edge and may come back as rivals.
- New stance **Barrage**: a ranged ninjutsu caster that keeps its distance, casts as often as chakra allows and holds back big area techniques near allies. See [Stances and squads](stances-and-squads.html).

## Update 141: stances and squads
- Drafted ninja get combat stances: Vanguard, Support, Assassin and Sealer change which techniques they use and how they move. Assassins earn an ambush bonus while unseen.
- Mark an enemy as the squad's priority target (right-click it), march drafted groups in formation, and post guards on patrol routes with hold fire.
- Medical ninja heal or carry downed allies on their own when it is safe. See [Stances and squads](stances-and-squads.html).

## Update 140: live squad missions
- Squad missions are planned from what your ninja can do (rank, elements, taijutsu, medics, sensors, genjutsu, summons) and can be watched in a live window with a map of the road and a log.
- What happens on the road comes home with them: wounds, loot, bounty shares, relics, hidden camps, fame, hunger and tiredness.
- Village mission rank: harder missions open as your village completes missions (B at level 2, A at 3, S at 4).
- Ten relics with small permanent bonuses for the whole village, and a Mission records window.
- New settings in the Living world section. See *Live squad missions*.

## Updates 133, 135, 137 and 139: the living world
- **The world moves on its own:** villages besiege, capture, raze and found settlements, sign ceasefires and go to war; named ninja of every village rank up, marry, have children and die; it all goes into the new **World news** in the Ninja world panel. See [The living world](living-world.html).
- **Squad missions:** send 1 to 4 ninja on a D- to S-rank mission off the map; they come back days later with a report.
- **Ninja society:** village leaders with attitudes and successions, genin teams, a chūnin exam host that rotates every year with named genin, envoys, hostages, tribute and marriage alliances, clans in your colony with heads, feuds and requests, and villages that remember what you did.
- **The living land:** travellers on the world map to trade with, escort, ambush or recruit; landmarks to explore; three legendary beasts (the Ashmaw, the Tidewyrm and the Thunderhorn) to hunt or befriend; pilgrims, monks, students and refugees at your door; a market that follows the wars.
- **Small touches:** festivals in other villages you can send a delegation to, letters from the ninja you know, and the stories of their deaths.
- Every part has its own switch under Mod settings > NFRP World > World and events > Living world. The legendary beasts borrow other creatures' art until their own is painted.

## Updates 134, 136 and 138: animation upgrade
- **Effects drawn in code:** shockwave rings, impact bursts, cones, beams, speed lines, ripples, lightning, slashes, domes, wind spirals, dust, sparks and smoke are now drawn in code: smooth every frame, sharp at any zoom, in the element's colours. Signature art stays painted. A new setting, **Procedural effects** (Full, Simple, Off), brings the painted animations back if you prefer them. See [Visual effects](visual-effects.html).
- **Impact feel:** a short pause on the heaviest hits and on breaks, dark (colour-inverting) flashes for shadow, ink and genjutsu, heat haze over fire and big impacts, debris that bounces and skids, and fading copies of a ninja along dashes.
- **The body:** throws, palms, slams, draws and grabs move the whole body, and the hands pull back before a strike. Kunai and senbon stay stuck where they hit; stone skin, frost, ink and seal tags show on the body.
- **Barriers and shots:** hostile shots slow down outside NFRP barriers, and wind barriers bend about half of them away (a gameplay setting, on by default).
- New settings for each part under Mod settings > NFRP World.

## Update 132: storytellers

Drawing only: damage and timing are unchanged.

## Updates 128 to 130: reputation, duels and intel
- **Witnesses:** deeds only become fame or infamy if someone who isn't one of yours lives to tell them; stories seen only by your prisoners wait until one goes free. See [Reputation, duels and intel](reputation-duels-and-intel.html).
- **Masked names:** a colonist in a masked-guard mask earns fame under the mask's name, with its own Bingo Book page; unmasked in front of outsiders, the names become one.
- **Infamy:** executions, harvesting captives, killing the helpless, betraying guests and allies earn a darker reputation: dark nicknames, more bounty hunters, less goodwill with the villages, harder intimidation, less inspiration.
- **Village renown:** your ninja's fame makes your village known: wanderers join, missions pay more, traders give a discount, raids grow a little. Shown in the Ninja world panel.
- **Duel challenges:** famous colonists get formal challenges (rivals too). Accept, and nobody else may step in.
- **Teachers and legacy:** famous sparring partners teach more; fallen legends leave the village their will and some renown; bury them, or thieves may come for the body.
- **Tales:** famous deeds become tales for your artists.
- **Body and bloodline bounties:** dead-only bounties, handing in bodies for the bounty, a legend's bloodline, clan ninja as prime targets.
- **Intel, spying and interrogation:** the Bingo Book fills in as you learn more; spy missions; interrogate prisoners for their secrets, camps you can raid and raids being planned.
- **Beheading a village:** kill or capture a village's leader and it is leaderless (and weaker) for a season.
- New settings for each part under Combat & AI.

## Updates 124 to 127: fame, the Bingo Book and rivals
- **Fame:** ninja, yours and the enemy's, build fame from kills, breaks, combos, won fights, missions and promotions. Known, Feared and Legendary ninja inspire allies and intimidate enemies near them in a fight, which also changes how fast break meters fill. See [Fame, the Bingo Book and rivals](fame-bingo-book-and-rivals.html).
- **Nicknames:** famous ninja earn a nickname from what they are known for, grander as they get more famous. A nickname you set yourself is never replaced.
- **Bingo Book:** a new button in the Ninja world panel. Famous ninja and their bounties; bounty hunters come for your most famous ninja, and you can hunt famous enemies for their bounty.
- **Rivals:** enemies who get away can come back for the ninja who hurt them, stronger, scarred and harder to beat the same way twice, leading a raid or alone for a duel.
- **New wing frames** for the 12 flying creatures, matching their new look.
- New settings under Combat & AI: **Fame**, **Inspire and intimidate strength**, **Weak enemies may flee**, **Earned nicknames**, **Bingo Book hunters**, **Rivals**.

- **New creature art:** 95 more creatures and summons repainted in the flatter base-game style. Flying creatures still flap with their old wings until their new wing frames arrive.
- Giddy-Up is listed as recommended, not required.
