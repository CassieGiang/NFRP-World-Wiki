# Saves and armour points
NFRP's Will and Fortitude saves and armour points, from the Player's Handbook: how strong techniques stick, and how much a ninja can take before being wounded.

@@summary A hostile technique's status, mind effect or stun rolls a save: d20 + Will or Fortitude against 10 + 2 per caster class. A Will save shrugs off a genjutsu; a Fortitude save halves a poison or stun. Every ninja also has armour points that take damage before wounds and come back with rest.

## Saves
When an enemy's technique puts a status, a mind effect or a stun on a pawn, the pawn rolls a save, as in the NFRP archive's techniques ("Will Save of 10 + 2 per Class Level"):

- **The roll:** d20 + the pawn's save, against **10 + 2 per caster class level** (D 1, C 2, B 3, A 4, S 5). So a D-class caster needs 12, an S-class caster 20.
- **Will:** genjutsu statuses and every mind effect a technique causes (fear, panic, berserk, charm). A pass **negates** it.
- **Fortitude:** every other technique's harmful status (poisons, paralysis, bleeding statuses) and stuns. A pass **halves** it: a timed status lasts half as long, a poison is half as strong, a stun is half as long.
- Only hostile techniques roll. Buffs, an ally's techniques and the technique's own marks, seals and links never do.
- A passed save shows over the pawn, e.g. "Will save 17 vs 14".

**Natural resistance by build and class** (the handbook's tables; D / C / B / A / S):

| Will | | Fortitude | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Taijutsu Master | 0 / 1 / 3 / 5 / 6 | Ninjutsu, Genjutsu or Clan Master | 0 / 1 / 3 / 5 / 6 |
| Other specialists | 1 / 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 | Other specialists | 1 / 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 |
| Genjutsu specialist or Ninjutsu Master | 2 / 3 / 5 / 8 / 10 | Taijutsu specialist | 2 / 3 / 5 / 8 / 10 |
| Genjutsu Master | 3 / 4 / 7 / 9 / 12 | Taijutsu Master | 3 / 4 / 7 / 9 / 12 |

A ninja without a [build](builds.html) uses the row of their strongest World of Naruto class. Someone who isn't a ninja counts as a D-class specialist (Will 1, Fortitude 1).

## Armour points
NFRP's "anime defence": the ability to take outrageous blows and keep fighting. Every ninja has a pool of armour points, shown in the health tab ("armour points 34 / 50") and the [Shinobi tab](fame-bingo-book-and-rivals.html#the-shinobi-tab).

- **They come first:** violent damage takes armour points before it wounds. 1 point soaks 0.2 damage. A blow bigger than what's left breaks through, less what the points took. A shield belt still takes a blow first.
- **How many:** by build and class points (the handbook's base armour points):

| Build | 1 class point | 5 | each point more | 20 (S class) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ninjutsu, Genjutsu or Clan Master | 20 | 40 | +4 | 100 (soaks 20) |
| Other specialists | 30 | 60 | +6 | 150 (soaks 30) |
| Taijutsu specialist | 40 | 80 | +8 | 200 (soaks 40) |
| Taijutsu Master | 50 | 100 | +10 | 250 (soaks 50) |

- **Rest:** as in the handbook's Rest and Recovery, an **extended rest** (6 hours asleep or meditating, once in 12 hours) brings them all back, and every **hour without being hit** brings back as many as the Fortitude save (at least 1).
- Enemy ninja have them too, so raids with ninja take longer to wear down.

## Settings
**Saves and armour points (NFRP)** in Mod settings → Village life → Progression turns both off. The test suite keeps them off outside its own section, "Test: saves and armour points (u522)".
