# World of Naruto techniques in our trees
World of Naruto's own techniques now sit in NFRP World's trees, with NFRP ranks, so there is one set of trees to learn from.

@@summary About 150 World of Naruto techniques moved into our trees with NFRP ranks; World of Naruto's 22 trees are hidden in the ninja tab. Duplicates use ours. Eye, bug and bloodline techniques still need their eye or gene. RimWorld-themed techniques have their own tree, Outworld Arts.

## Where they went
- **Basic ninjutsu** goes to the universal trees (Shape Transformation, Universal Ninjutsu, Summoning, Shunshin, Kage Bunshin), to Binding and Cursed seals, to Medical Ninjutsu and to the Will of Kratos.
- **Element techniques** go to our arts trees (Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Lightning, Wood). Ice and Sand techniques go to our Ice and Sand arts and stay bloodline only.
- **Genjutsu** goes to Universal Genjutsu and the four schools; **taijutsu** to the universal taijutsu trees; Morning Peacock and Hirudora to the Eight Gates.
- **Uchiha, Hyuuga and Aburame** techniques go to our Uchiha, Hyuuga and Aburame arts. They still need the eye or host: a Mangekyō technique needs that Mangekyō, Sharingan techniques the Sharingan. The Learn window says "needs the Sharingan" and so on. The eye itself comes free with the gene.
- **RimWorld-themed techniques** (Anima, Hemogen, mechanitor, Thrumbo and teleport arts) have a tree of their own: **Gaikai no Jutsu (Outworld Arts)**, open to everyone.

In each tree our techniques come first and the moved ones sit in rows below them. World of Naruto's trees are hidden in the ninja tab but still exist, so genes, natures and items that open them keep working. The Jinchūriki and Animal trees and weapon techniques are untouched.

## Duplicates use ours
Where World of Naruto has a technique NFRP World already has, ours is used and World of Naruto's button goes away once ours is learned. For example: Mystical Palm → Shōsen no Jutsu (Healing Hands), Chakra Scalpel → Chakra no Mesu, Phoenix Flower → Katon: Hōsenka no Jutsu, Dragon Flame → Katon: Karyūdan (Fire Dragon Missile), Sleep, Fear and Invisibility → our genjutsu, Rotation → Hakkeshō Kaiten (Eight Trigrams Great Divination Whirl), Eight Inner Gates → our Eight Gates. Body Flicker and Shadow Clone are not duplicates: they moved into Shunshin and Kage Bunshin (Shadow Clone stays a rare art).

## Ranks
Each moved technique has its NFRP (canon) rank, which sets its price in [Training Points](training-points.html). Fifteen were moved off canon to match the chakra cost of our techniques of that rank: for example Adamantite Chains, Exploding Water Shockwave and Sharingan Genjutsu World are S, Seal Senses, Water Prison and Summon Swarm A, Clear Sky A, and Crow Substitute and Fire Absorption C. World of Naruto's own numbers (damage, chakra, cooldown) are unchanged.
