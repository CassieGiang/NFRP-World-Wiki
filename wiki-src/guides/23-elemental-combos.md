# Elemental combos
Two ninja hitting the same spot with different elements mix them into a combo.

@@summary Two of your ninja hitting the same spot with different elements within a few seconds mix them into a combo, such as Firestorm, Electrified water or Mudslide. It works with any technique from any tree.

When two of your ninja hit the same spot with **different elements** within about five seconds, the elements mix into a combo. It works with area techniques and single-target strikes alike, from any tree. Both casters must be in the same faction. A combo shows its name over the spot and hits **everyone** in the area (about 3 cells across each way) except the two ninja who made it, so mind your own people. Chain lightning is the exception: it only jumps between enemies. After a combo, the same area can't combo again for a few seconds.

Blade techniques count as wind, and dust as earth.

## The combos
- **Fire + wind: Firestorm.** 9 flame damage, and a few small fires start.
- **Water + lightning: Electrified water.** 6 lightning damage and a stun of about 2.5 seconds.
- **Earth + water: Mudslide.** 4 blunt damage, and everyone is stuck in mud (40% slower) for 20 seconds.
- **Wind + water: Squall.** 5 blunt damage, and everyone is knocked back 2 cells.
- **Fire + water: Scalding steam.** 5 burn damage and a cloud of blinding steam.
- **Lightning + wind: Chain lightning.** Jumps through up to 5 nearby enemies, 10 lightning damage falling to 6, with a short stun.
- **Earth + fire: Magma burst.** 11 flame damage, and small fires in the middle.
- **Ice + wind: Blizzard.** 5 frostbite damage, and everyone is chilled (30% slower, 15% less manipulation) for 25 seconds.
- **Sand + wind: Sandstorm.** 4 cut damage and a cloud of blinding sand.

## Elemental advantages
When two natures meet head-on, one can overpower the other: **fire beats wind, wind beats lightning, lightning beats earth, earth beats water, and water beats fire.**

- **Clashes.** Two techniques clash when their shots meet in the air, or when two ninja cast elemental attacks at each other within about a second. The stronger nature wins: it carries on at **half damage** and the other is gone ("overpowers!"). The same nature, or two that don't touch in the cycle (fire and lightning, say), cancel each other ("cancelled out!").
- **Rank beats nature.** A technique two or more ranks higher wins whatever the natures, as Naruto's Rasenshuriken did. One rank isn't enough.
- **Advanced natures count by their parts:** ice is wind + water, wood is earth + water, lava is fire + earth, explosion is earth + lightning, sand and magnet are wind + earth, storm is lightning + water, boil is fire + water and scorch is fire + wind. Every clash compares every part against every part, and the side with more winning pairings wins; a tie cancels. So ice beats lightning (its wind), lightning beats lava (its earth), and ice against fire cancels (water beats fire, fire beats wind).
- **Walls.** A barrier wall (earth, water, wind, lightning, wood, ice or sand) takes **double damage** from shots of a nature that beats it, so lightning breaks an earth wall twice as fast.
- **Plain shots** with no nature in the cycle (kunai, shuriken, chakra blasts, bugs, paper, ink) don't clash this way: they flash and fly on, as before.

You can turn this off in the mod settings (Progression: **Elemental advantages**).
