# Jutsu chains and solo combos
Trained ninja can fire several techniques on one order, and finish elemental combos on their own. Both open up with training, and a letter tells you the moment a ninja can do something new.

## Jutsu chains
- A ninja fires up to **three ninjutsu back to back** on one order.
- **Steps:** one at first; a second step at **Ninjutsu 10**, a third at **Ninjutsu 16**.
- The first step has its normal wind-up. Later steps take half of it, because the hand seals are already flowing, and less as the ninja masters it (down to about a third at Ninjutsu 20). The gap between steps shrinks with skill too.
- Each step pays its own chakra and goes on its own cooldown. A step still on cooldown is skipped.
- The chain stops when chakra runs out, the ninja is downed or stunned, or no enemy is left. If the target dies, the next step goes to the nearest enemy.
- Only offensive ninjutsu aimed at an enemy can go in a chain: no taijutsu, toggles, summons, heals or buffs.
- Set the chain in the combo window's **Jutsu chain** tab, then fire it with the **Jutsu chain** button on the drafted ninja.

## Break combo slots
- The **Break combo** tab holds the techniques a ninja follows up with after breaking a foe's guard (see [break and combos](break-and-combos.html)).
- Slots follow the rank-exam levels: **2** below level 8, **3** from level 8, **4** from 13, **5** from 16 or for a village leader.

## Solo combos
Two allied ninja whose elements meet on a target make an elemental combo: fire and wind make a firestorm, fire and water scalding steam, and so on (see [elemental combos](elemental-combos.html)). A single ninja can now do it alone by casting the second element onto what the first left behind.
- **Training in an element** comes from the techniques a ninja knows in it: S 5 points, A 4, B 3, C 2, D 1, and +2 for their chakra nature.
- **4 points in each** of the two elements: a light solo combo (about 60% strength, smaller area).
- **9 points in each:** the full combo, as strong as two ninja working together.
- A **bloodline that fuses the two elements** (lava for fire and earth, boil for fire and water, and others) always counts as fully trained.
- The first element's mark now lasts 5.5 seconds, so one ninja has time for the second cast. The two hits must be two casts, not one technique's own burst.
- A jutsu chain of fire then water from a trained ninja makes their own scalding steam.

## Letters
About every 30 seconds of game time the game checks your ninja. A new chain step, a new combo slot or a new solo combo arrives in one letter, with how to use it. A new recruit gets one letter listing what they can already do.
