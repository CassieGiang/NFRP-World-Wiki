# The academy
How colonist children become genin: studying, awakening chakra, Academy techniques and the graduation exam. Needs **Biotech** (children and the learning need).

@@summary Children aged 6 to 17 with no rank are academy students. Studying awakens their chakra and earns 5 TP a day; from 13, with 200 TP earned, they sit the academy graduation exam and become genin. Academy techniques are free from genin on.

## Who is a student
Every colonist child aged **6 to 17** with no ninja rank is an academy student. There is no building or job to set up: a child studies whenever they fill Biotech's **learning need**: lessons, the school desk, reading, play and the rest.

Select a student to see their academy line on the inspect panel: what they earned today and the age they can graduate.

## Chakra
A student without chakra **awakens it the first time they learn**, and a letter says so. From then on they can learn and use techniques. See [Becoming a ninja](becoming-a-ninja.html#awakening-chakra).

## Training Points
- A student earns **5 TP once a day** from lessons, the school desk and other learning, like a ninja class. See [Training Points](training-points.html#earning-tp).
- **Lessons from a ranked ninja** (a genin or higher teaching within 8 cells) count double when Training Points are off.
- With Training Points off, studying gives class XP instead: up to 40 a day, and only up to class level 5. Past that, training and missions take over.

## Academy techniques
The Academy tree is in the Ninjutsu tab and open to everyone:
- Kinobori no Waza (Tree Climbing)
- Suimen Hokō no Waza (Water Walking)
- Kawarimi no Jutsu (Body Replacement Technique)
- Jibaku Fuda: Kassei (Exploding Tag): a real blast, see [More arts](more-arts.html#body-flicker-the-counter-and-blasts)
- Kishōdoku (Chakra antiseptic)
- Kijōsui (Chakra water purify)
- Sennen Goroshi (Thousand Years of Pain)

A student pays their TP price. From **genin** on they are **free**, and any TP a student paid for them comes back after graduating, with a message. They never count toward the [ninja build](builds.html).

## The graduation exam
A child's first rank exam is the **academy graduation exam**:
- from age **13**,
- with **200 TP earned** (with Training Points off: ninja level 3).

It runs with the colony's [rank exam](ranks.html), and a child gets an easier roll than an adult sitting the genin exam. Passing makes them a **genin** with a good mood for ten days ("graduated from the academy", +8). A failed exam can be tried again at the next one.

Adults with no rank sit the ordinary genin exam instead.

## Other villages
The villages' named ninja grow up the same way: their academy children become genin at 12, and academy graduates aged 12 to 15 join the roster as it fills. See [The living world](living-world.html).
