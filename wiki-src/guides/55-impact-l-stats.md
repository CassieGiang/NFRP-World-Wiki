# IMPACT-L stats
NFRP's seven Ability Categories, from the Player's Handbook: Intelligence, Might, Perception, Agility, Charisma, Toughness and Luck. They don't make a ninja smarter or more charming in the story; they move numbers.

@@summary Each ninja gets 1 point per class reached (D, C, B, A, S: 5 in all) to place in a letter. Every 2 points in a letter give one step of its bonus. Handseals, movements and the Spring Time of Youth pool come from the build and class, and the letters add to them.

## Points
- **1 point for every class reached:** D (at once), C, B, A and S. **5 in all.**
- Place them in the **Stats** tab of the [Learn techniques](training-points.html) window ("Stats (1)" when one is waiting). A placed point stays until a respec.
- **Steps of 2:** every bonus comes per 2 points in a letter, so an odd point waits for its pair.
- A **respec** gives all the placed points back.
- **Enemy and allied ninja** place theirs by role, in pairs: taijutsu fighters Might, Might, Agility, Agility, Toughness; genjutsu users Perception, Perception, Charisma, Charisma, Perception; medics Intelligence, Intelligence, Toughness, Toughness, Intelligence; ninjutsu users and everyone else Intelligence, Intelligence, Agility, Agility, Intelligence.
- An older colony gets one letter when this arrives: its ninja have their points to place.
- The [Shinobi tab](fame-bingo-book-and-rivals.html#the-shinobi-tab) shows the letters in one line, e.g. "I2 M0 P0 A2 C0 T1 L0".

## What each step gives
| Letter | Per step of 2 points | Every 2 steps |
|---|---|---|
| **I** Intelligence | +5 handseals, +3% chakra damage | +1 Intellectual |
| **M** Might | +5% melee damage, +1 break pressure per blow, +5% carrying | +1 Mining and Construction |
| **P** Perception | +1 Will save, +5% sensing range | +1 Shooting |
| **A** Agility | +1 movement, +0.05 move speed | +1 Melee |
| **C** Charisma | +1 to the difficulty of your own Will-save techniques | +1 Social |
| **T** Toughness | +5 armour points, +1 Fortitude save, +1 Spring Time of Youth point | |
| **L** Luck | +5% learning, +1 melee dodge (about +2%), +2% on luck rolls | |

**Luck rolls:** a chakra paper slip showing a new nature, awakening chakra at the apparatus (the student's or the sensei's Luck, whichever is higher), and the scrolls, seals and blades a mission, a ruin or a casket gives. A squad mission uses the squad's best Luck; quest offers, ruins and caskets use the colony's best. Luck only raises a chance that exists: it never adds a prize a rank doesn't have.

See [saves and armour points](saves-and-armour-points.html) for Will, Fortitude and armour points.

## Class
With IMPACT-L on, World of Naruto's per-level bonuses of the Ninjutsu, Taijutsu and Genjutsu classes and of the element trees are off. Instead every ninja gets, per class level (D 1 ... S 5): **+15 max chakra** and **4% faster casting**. An S-class ninja has +75 chakra and casts 20% faster.

## Handseals
Every ninja makes a number of handseals per turn (the handbook's table), +5 per Intelligence step:

| Build | D | C | B | A | S |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ninjutsu, Genjutsu or Clan Master | 25 | 50 | 75 | 100 | 125 |
| Other specialists | 20 | 40 | 60 | 80 | 100 |
| Taijutsu specialist | 15 | 30 | 45 | 60 | 75 |
| Taijutsu Master | 10 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 |

A technique needs D 6, C 12, B 18, A 24 or S 30 handseals (less a quarter or a third for specialists at higher classes). This sets the hand-sign wind-up of ninjutsu, genjutsu and clan techniques:
- A technique of the ninja's **own class rank** winds up as before.
- A **lower** rank winds up faster, a **higher** rank slower, and **Intelligence** makes every one faster.
- Never faster than half or slower than double. Taijutsu needs no seals.

## Movements
Every ninja has a number of movements per turn, +1 per Agility step:

| Build | D | C | B | A | S |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ninjutsu, Genjutsu or Clan Master | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| Other specialists | 3 | 5 | 6 | 8 | 9 |
| Taijutsu specialist | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 | 12 |
| Taijutsu Master | 5 | 8 | 10 | 13 | 16 |

- Each movement above 3 gives **+4% melee attack speed**.
- **Jutsu chain steps:** 5 movements open a second step, 8 a third (see [jutsu chains](jutsu-chains-and-solo-combos.html)).
- **Break combo slots** follow the class: D 2, C 3, B 4, A and S 5, and a village leader 5.

## Spring Time of Youth
Every ninja has a Spring Time of Youth pool, shown in the health tab:

| Build | D | C | B | A | S |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ninjutsu, Genjutsu or Clan Master | 2 | 3 | 5 | 7 | 9 |
| Other specialists | 3 | 5 | 7 | 9 | 12 |
| Taijutsu specialist | 4 | 6 | 9 | 12 | 15 |
| Taijutsu Master | 5 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 |

+1 per Toughness step. The handbook's own table for it is lost, so this one is the mod's, built like the others.
- **Spend 1 point** with the **Spring Time of Youth** button on the ninja: an **extra melee attack** at once (the next blow comes without the pause), **3 seconds of +20% move and melee speed**, or **3 seconds of +20% dodge**. Youth techniques keep their own costs.
- **Below half** the pool, the ninja is **fatigued**: -10% move and melee speed.
- **8 hours of sleep or meditation** fill it. Meditation techniques still add to it during the day.

## Gear, elixirs and Unlock Potential
These add to the placed points and count past the 5. A respec doesn't touch them. Only ninja get them.

| Source | Bonus | Made by | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Harnessed gem** | +6 to one letter | someone who knows Bagushi (Harness expert), at the alchemy table (gem harnessing) | Worn on the belt; one belt item at a time. Ruby or garnet Might, sapphire Intelligence, emerald or jade Luck, topaz or turquoise Agility, onyx Toughness, amethyst or opal Charisma, moonstone or pearl Perception |
| **Letter ring** (e.g. the luck ring) | +6 to its letter | someone who knows Kyōka Yubiwa (Enhanced Rings), at the alchemy table | Worn on the right hand; one ring at a time |
| **Elixir** of a letter | +4 for a day | the alchemy table (ninja alchemy) | A second dose starts the day again |
| **Unlock Potential** | +8 to every letter, +8 saves, +8 movements, 60 seconds | its [forbidden scroll](rare-arts-and-legendary-blades.html) | Afterwards the Spring Time of Youth is spent |

A gem and a ring can be worn together: a luck ring and a harnessed emerald make Luck 12 (6 steps). The Stats tab shows where each bonus comes from.

## Settings
**IMPACT-L stats (NFRP)** in Mod settings → Village life → Progression turns it off. World of Naruto's class and element bonuses come back after a restart.

## Ability Damage and Drain
Some techniques take IMPACT-L points away.

- **Ability damage** is lost for a while: it comes back at 1 point a day in each letter.
- **Ability drain** stays until it's cleansed.
- **Steps:** losses count in 2s, like bonuses: every 2 points lost in a letter take away one step (for example, 2 Toughness lost means 5 fewer armour points and 1 less Fortitude). Anyone can be hit, ninja or not; someone without points simply goes below 0.
- **At −15** in a letter the person is helpless until enough comes back:
  - Intelligence: a coma.
  - Might: lying helpless on the ground.
  - Perception: a deep sleep full of nightmares.
  - Agility: rigid and motionless.
  - Charisma: a catatonic stupor.
  - Toughness: death.
  - Luck at −15 does nothing more than the lost steps.

**What deals it:**
- **Soul Drain:** 1d4+1 points of one letter. The ninja has a button to pick the letter; otherwise it takes the target's weakest. A Will save negates it.
- **Psychic Blast:** 1d4 Intelligence and 1d4 Perception.
- **Wooden Blight:** 1d4 Agility every round while it holds; the round its save ends it, half. At Agility −15 the victim becomes an inert wooden statue.
- **Red Tide:** a Fortitude save or 3d6 Might, and a minute later another save or 3d6 more.
- **Deadly Stroke:** 1 Toughness against a stunned, sleeping, downed or flat-footed foe (3 from B class).
- **Disruptive Spring Time of Youth:** 1 point of Might, Agility or Toughness, picked with the same kind of button.
- **The Crippling seal:** 1d3 Might when it triggers.
- **The Infectious seal:** 1d3 Toughness when it triggers.
- **Death Plume gems:** 1d3 Toughness to everyone in the ash.
- **Chakra Consumption:** −4 Toughness on the user while wracked.

**What restores it:**
- **Cleanse:** Poison 2 points, Disease 4, Panacea all damage and all drain.
- **Trauma Care:** 1d4 points (1d8 from B class; from A class, every point of the worst-hit letter).
- **A wondrous lifecap** used in a tend: 2 points.
- **Mind Over Body:** heals 1 + Indomitable Fortitude points a day.
- **Curse of Mortality:** a day's recovery only comes with a Fortitude save.
- **Battle Through Pain:** losses up to the ninja's Impeccable Will don't count, but it can't stop death.
- **Exoskeleton of Undeath:** better saves against Might, Agility and Toughness damage.
- **Alchemical Perfection:** immune to drain.
