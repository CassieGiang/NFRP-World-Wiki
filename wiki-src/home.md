# NFRP World
A RimWorld mod that brings the clans, bloodlines and techniques of the Naruto Forum Role-play (NFRP) archive into **World of Naruto**: more than 150 technique trees, ninja builds, hidden villages at war, ranked missions, summons, alchemy, seals and more.

**Requires:** [Harmony](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077), [Taran Magic Framework](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3527502876), [World of Naruto](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3025338958) and [Giddy-Up](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3674332861), loaded in that order, then NFRP World. Works on RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7.
